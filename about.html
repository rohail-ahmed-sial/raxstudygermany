About RAX Study Germany

A free, open-source study and visa-preparation site.

RAX Study Germany is a free, open-source website for people preparing to move to Germany. It started as a personal study guide for the Goethe-Zertifikat A2 writing exam and grew into five parts:

  • German A1 and A2Exam patterns, Lesen, Hören, Schreiben and Sprechen practice, grammar, 1,506 words with English meanings, flashcards and mock tests.Learn
  • IELTS AcademicOriginal practice for all four sections, a band calculator and a mock-test generator.Practise
  • Germany visa guidePlain-language guides to each visa route, with official sources, checklists and a planner.Prepare
  • Bachelor’s in GermanyWhat 12 or 13 years of education from Pakistan mean for admission, the Studienkolleg, and how to apply.Study
  • Document attestationBoard, IBCC, HEC and MOFA procedures for Pakistani documents, with a route selector and a personal checklist.Attest

Who made it

RAX Study Germany is made by Rohail Ahmed Sial. You can reach me on GitHub, LinkedIn or by email. Corrections are very welcome, especially when an official page has changed.

Open source

The full source is on GitHub. The code is released under the MIT License. The original learning content (practice texts, model answers, explanations) is released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): you may reuse it if you credit RAX Study Germany. Exam names and trademarks belong to their owners.

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework and no build step. You can open it from a folder on your computer, host it on Netlify or GitHub Pages, and edit the visa figures in one data file.

How content is checked

Exam formats were checked against the Goethe-Institut and the IELTS partners. Visa information was checked against the German Missions in Pakistan, the Federal Foreign Office and the federal government’s Make it in Germany portal, and attestation steps against IBCC, HEC, MOFA and the HCCH, on 29 October 2026. Each visa figure has its own source, check date and status. The site does not update itself, so check the official page before you rely on any figure. See all sources.

What RAX Study Germany does not do

  • It does not search for, compare or rank universities or degree programmes.
  • It does not check your admission eligibility or manage applications.
  • It does not submit anything to any authority, book appointments or offer paid services.
  • It is not an application tracker: checklist ticks are your own notes, never the status of an IBCC, HEC, MOFA or visa application.
  • It is not connected to IBCC, HEC, MOFA, the Goethe-Institut, IELTS, the German government or any German mission.

Contribute

Found a mistake or an outdated figure? Open an issue on GitHub with the page, the official link and what changed. Pull requests with new practice material are welcome; see CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository.
