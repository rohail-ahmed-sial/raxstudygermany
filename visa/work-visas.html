Germany work visas compared

Which work route fits you: job offer, recognition, salary and language compared.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Germany has several work routes. Which one fits depends on your qualification, whether it is recognised, whether you have a job offer, and your salary. This table gives an overview; each guide has the details.

Steps that most work routes share

  1. Check your qualificationFind out whether your profession is regulated in Germany and whether your qualification needs recognition. Use the official Recognition in Germany portal; for degrees, check anabin.
  2. Find a jobYour employer fills in the declaration of employment (Erklärung zum Beschäftigungsverhältnis) that the authorities use.
  3. ApprovalFor many routes the Federal Employment Agency must approve the job. Employers can speed this up with the accelerated procedure for skilled workers (§ 81a), which gives a pre-approval.
  4. Apply for the visaFrom Pakistan, most work routes use the Consular Services Portal (digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt). With a § 81a pre-approval you can get a priority appointment.
  5. After arrivalRegister your address and apply for your residence permit (or EU Blue Card) at the foreigners’ authority.

Applying from Pakistan

The German Missions in Pakistan handle the EU Blue Card, skilled workers, vocational training, recognition measures, the Opportunity Card, au pairs and specialty cooks through the Consular Services Portal. Research, self-employment and voluntary service are not on the portal; you register for a regular appointment. Check the mission’s page for your category before you start.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Regulated professions

In regulated professions, such as doctors, nurses and teachers, you need recognition or a licence to practise before you can work in that profession. A job offer alone is not enough. The recognition routes can help you complete missing parts in Germany.
