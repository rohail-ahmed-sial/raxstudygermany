Ausbildung visa (vocational training)

Vocational training in Germany: contract, B1 German, money, and applying from Islamabad or Karachi.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 16a AufenthG (vocational training). Seeking a training place: § 17(1).
For
Company-based (dual) training with a contract, or school-based training at a vocational school.
Language
Normally German B1.
Approval
The Federal Employment Agency (Bundesagentur für Arbeit) usually has to approve company-based training.
Money
Company-based: training allowance of at least €1,048 gross per month Verified. School-based: €959 per month Verified.
Apply in Pakistan
Islamabad: online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt. Karachi: the Consulate General’s page says applications continue in person for now.
Visa fee
€75 Verified

How vocational training works

An Ausbildung usually lasts two to three and a half years. In dual training you work at a company and attend a vocational school; you sign a training contract and receive a monthly allowance. In school-based training (for example in some health and social care professions) you learn mainly at a school.

Requirements

  • A training contract with a company, or a confirmed place at a vocational school.
  • German at B1 in most cases. The company or school may ask for more.
  • Approval by the Federal Employment Agency for company-based training (the company and the authorities arrange this).
  • Money for your living costs: the training allowance if it is high enough (€1,048 gross per month in 2026), otherwise additional proof. For school-based training: €959 per month.
  • Your school-leaving certificates, with translations if required.

Documents

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Vocational training contract or school confirmation
    Company-based (dual) training or school-based training.
  • German language certificate (normally B1)
  • School-leaving certificates
    With translations if required.
  • Proof of financing
    Training allowance (€1,048 gross in 2026) or €959 per month for school-based training.
  • Health insurance
    Until statutory insurance starts with the training.
  • CV
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

Seeking a training place

If you do not have a training contract yet, the visa for seeking a training place (§ 17(1)) lets you come to Germany to look for one for up to nine months. The conditions are stricter:

  • You are under 35.
  • You have German at B1.
  • You have a school-leaving certificate that gives access to higher education in Germany, or one from a German school abroad.
  • You can show €1,091 per month Verified for the whole stay.

How to apply from Pakistan

  1. Find a training placeApply to companies or schools. The Federal Employment Agency and the Make it in Germany portal list training places.
  2. Reach B1 GermanTake a recognised exam such as Goethe, telc or ÖSD.
  3. Get the contract and approvalYour employer signs the contract; approval is arranged with the authorities.
  4. ApplyIslamabad: online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt. Karachi: in person for now, as the Consulate General’s page says. Pay the fee in PKR.
  5. Travel and registerRegister your address after arrival and apply for your residence permit before the visa expires.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Common mistakes

  • Applying with A2 when the route expects B1.
  • A contract without the details the authorities need (pay, hours, start date).
  • Paying a recruitment agent for a “guaranteed” place. Check every offer with the company itself.

After your training

After a successful Ausbildung in Germany you can usually look for a job matching your qualification and then switch to a residence permit as a skilled worker. See qualified professionals.

What this visa does not allow

  • Working outside your training beyond what the rules allow.
  • Changing to a different training programme without informing the foreigners’ authority.
