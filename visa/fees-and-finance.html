Money you show and money you pay

  • Money you show (proof of financing) stays your money. For students it is usually paid into a blocked account and released to you month by month in Germany.
  • Money you pay includes the visa fee, attestation fees, translations, insurance, the blocked account provider’s fees and your flight.
  • Visa fees at the German Missions in Pakistan are paid in cash in Pakistani rupees, at the exchange rate the mission sets.

Ways to prove financing

  • Blocked account (Sperrkonto): any provider accepted by the German authorities. When opening from Pakistan, a signature attestation may be needed (€25 (about PKR 5,400) Verified).
  • Formal obligation letter (Verpflichtungserklärung): signed by someone in Germany at their foreigners’ authority.
  • Scholarship: for students, the missions accept HEC scholarships (see the student visa guide).
  • Salary or training allowance: for work and company-based training, the contract can show that you can support yourself.
