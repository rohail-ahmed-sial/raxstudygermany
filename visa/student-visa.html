Germany student visa from Pakistan

Admission basics, the 2026 blocked account amount, documents and the online application from Pakistan.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 16b AufenthG (studies). Seeking a study place: § 17(2).
For
People admitted to a state-recognised university, a Studienkolleg (foundation course) or a preparatory language course.
Work allowed
140 full or 280 half days a year, or 20 hours a week Verified.
Money to show
€992 per month Verified, that is €11,904 per year Verified (2026).
Language
The language requirement of your programme, in German or English.
Apply in Pakistan
Online through the Consular Services Portal at digital.diplo.de/studium, then an appointment.
Visa fee
€75 Verified

Who this visa is for

The student visa is for a full degree programme (bachelor’s, master’s or PhD as a student), a Studienkolleg that prepares you for a degree, or a preparatory course such as a German course that leads into your studies. You need a place first: either an admission letter or a conditional admission.

Admission basics for a bachelor’s degree

This section explains admission only as far as the visa needs it. For the full guide, read Bachelor’s in Germany, including the 13-year route (HSSC plus one year at university) and the Studienkolleg. RAX Study Germany does not search for, compare or rank universities and is not an admission service.

  1. Check your school certificateTo study in Germany you need a qualification that gives access to higher education (Hochschulzugangsberechtigung, HZB). The anabin database of the Standing Conference of education ministers shows how a foreign certificate is classified, and universities or uni-assist make the final decision.
  2. Direct access or StudienkollegFor Pakistan, the Higher Secondary School Certificate (Intermediate, HSSC) on its own usually does not give direct access to a bachelor’s degree. The usual paths are a Studienkolleg ending with the assessment test (Feststellungsprüfung), or successful study at a recognised university in Pakistan first. Which path applies depends on your subject and grades. Needs review Confirm in anabin and with the university.
  3. Studienkolleg coursesCourses are grouped by subject area, for example T (engineering and science), M (medicine and biology), W (business and social sciences), G (humanities) and S (languages). The entrance test usually expects German at about B1, sometimes B2.
  4. Language for the degreeProgrammes taught in German usually require about C1 (for example TestDaF or DSH). Programmes taught in English set their own minimum, often IELTS or TOEFL. Check each programme page.
  5. Apply to the universityApply directly to the university or through uni-assist, depending on the university. Deadlines and documents differ by university and programme.
  6. CostsMost public universities charge a semester contribution. Some federal states charge tuition fees for students from outside the EU. Check the university’s fee page and, if you pay tuition, keep the payment proof for your visa application.

Useful official starting points: DAAD, anabin and uni-assist.

Requirements for the visa

  • Admission or conditional admission from a state-recognised university, Studienkolleg or preparatory course.
  • Proof that you can pay for your stay (see below).
  • Proof that you meet the language requirement of your programme, unless the admission letter already confirms it.
  • Health insurance for the start of your stay, then student health insurance in Germany.
  • Your school certificates and any university degrees and transcripts. The German Missions in Pakistan say these, and proof of tuition payment if your programme charges fees, must be uploaded from the start.

Proving you can pay for your studies

For 2026 the required amount is €992 per month Verified, which is €11,904 per year Verified for one year. You can usually prove it in one of these ways:

  • Blocked account (Sperrkonto). You pay the yearly amount into an account in Germany and can withdraw a fixed amount each month after arrival. When you open one from Pakistan, your signature may need to be attested: the missions list a fee of €25 (about PKR 5,400) Verified at the Embassy, the Consulate General or the Honorary Consul in Lahore.
  • Formal obligation letter (Verpflichtungserklärung). Someone living in Germany signs at their local foreigners’ authority that they will cover your costs.
  • Scholarship. The missions’ financing page says only scholarships from the Higher Education Commission (HEC) are accepted, not scholarships granted by universities. If you hold another scholarship, check the current page or ask the mission.

Seeking a study place

If you have not been admitted yet, the visa for seeking a study place (§ 17(2)) lets you come to Germany to apply for and prepare for a degree. It is issued for up to nine months. You need a school-leaving certificate that qualifies you for a university or Studienkolleg, the language level your planned programmes require, and money of €1,091 per month Verified for the whole stay. Part-time work is limited. Ask the mission whether it currently processes this category before you prepare.

Documents

This is a starting list. Download the latest checklist of the German Missions in Pakistan before you upload anything.

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Admission letter or conditional admission
    From a state-recognised German university, Studienkolleg or preparatory course.
  • Previous degree certificates and transcripts
    School certificates (Matric, Intermediate) and any university degrees: mandatory from the start of the online application.
  • Proof of tuition payment, if your programme charges fees
    Mandatory if applicable.
  • Proof of financing
    Blocked account confirmation (€992 per month in 2026), formal obligation letter (Verpflichtungserklärung) or HEC scholarship.
  • Language certificate
    German or English, as required by the programme.
  • Health insurance
    Travel or incoming insurance until you enrol; then statutory or private student insurance in Germany.
  • CV and motivation letter, if requested
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

How to apply from Pakistan

  1. Get your admissionAdmission or conditional admission from your university, Studienkolleg or preparatory course.
  2. Arrange financingOpen a blocked account, or arrange a formal obligation letter or an accepted scholarship.
  3. Prepare documents and copiesThe Embassy cannot legalise Pakistani civil documents. It offers certified copies of educational documents in Islamabad (online appointment) and Karachi (fixed opening hours); see the missions’ Legal page for the current rules. For board, IBCC and HEC attestation, see the document attestation guide.
  4. Apply onlineFill in the questionnaire on digital.diplo.de/studium and upload your documents. The mission checks your application and invites you to an appointment.
  5. Attend the appointmentBring your passport, the original documents and the fee. Your fingerprints are taken.
  6. Wait for the decisionYou are told when to collect your passport. Do not book non-refundable flights before you have the visa.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading an incomplete application: certificates, transcripts and tuition proof (if any) are needed from the start.
  • Showing last year’s blocked account amount. The amount changes, usually on 1 January.
  • Paying an agent for a place in the queue. The portal and appointments are free; only the visa fee is paid at the mission.
  • Bringing an unsigned passport. The missions cannot issue a visa in a passport you have not signed.
  • Relying on a scholarship that the mission does not accept as proof of financing.

After arrival

  1. Register your addressRegister at the local citizens’ office (Bürgeramt) within two weeks of moving into your flat.
  2. Enrol and insureEnrol at the university and take out student health insurance.
  3. Get your residence permitApply at the foreigners’ authority before your visa expires.
  4. After graduationYou can stay up to 18 months to look for a job that matches your degree (§ 20(1) no. 1). Graduates of German universities can apply for a settlement permit after two years as a skilled worker, with 24 months of pension contributions and German at B1.

What this visa does not allow

  • Working more than the yearly limit of 140 full or 280 half days a year, or 20 hours a week without approval.
  • Using the visa mainly for full-time employment.
  • Bringing family members automatically. Each family member needs a visa and must meet the requirements for family reunification.
