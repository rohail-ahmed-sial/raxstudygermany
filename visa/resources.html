German Missions in Pakistan: Visa – short-term / Schengen
Free online registration on a waiting list (Islamabad and Karachi), one appointment per person, apply up to six months before travel, Videx form, processing usually about 14 calendar days. Anyone asking for money for registration is a fraudster. Article dated 19.02.2026.		Schengen visa procedurePakistanVerified29 October 2026
German Missions in Pakistan: Legal
Pakistani civil status documents cannot be attested or legalised by the Embassy. Certified copies of educational documents possible at Islamabad and Karachi (no HEC cross-attestation needed for this service; one set free with proof of intent to study). No sworn translators in Pakistan. Blocked account release letter attestation €36. Page dated 14.04.2026.		Documents, legalisation, translationsPakistanVerified29 October 2026
