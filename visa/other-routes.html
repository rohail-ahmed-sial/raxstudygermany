Au pair, voluntary service and internships

Au pair, voluntary service and study-related internships.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Au pair

For
Young people who live with a host family, help with childcare and learn German.
Age
18–27, as listed by the German Missions in Pakistan.
Language
German A1.
Host family
Not related to you, not of your country of origin, with a child under 18.
Pocket money
Verify current requirement Needs review
Apply in Pakistan
Online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt.
  • A valid au pair contract with the host family.
  • You have not been an au pair in Germany before.
  • The host family usually arranges health, accident and liability insurance.

Au pair checklist

Voluntary service

For a voluntary service in Germany you need a contract with a recognised provider: the Federal Volunteer Service (BFD), a Voluntary Social Year (FSJ), a Voluntary Ecological Year (FÖJ) or the European Voluntary Service. From Pakistan, register for a regular appointment; this category is not on the online portal.

Voluntary service checklist

Study-related internship

  • Legal basis: § 16e AufenthG.
  • You are enrolled at a university abroad, or graduated within the last two years.
  • The internship matches your field of study.
  • The host covers your living and travel costs.
  • Up to six months. You cannot take other jobs on this visa.
