Looking for work in Germany: routes compared

Opportunity Card, study-place and training-place search compared.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

There is no single “job seeker visa” any more. Since June 2024 the Opportunity Card has replaced the earlier job-seeker visa for skilled workers. Depending on what you are looking for, these routes let you search in Germany:

RouteWho it is forMaximum stayMoney (2026)LanguageWork while searching
Opportunity Card (§ 20a)Skilled people looking for a jobUp to 1 year€1,091 per monthA1 German or B2 English (points route)Up to 20 hours a week, trial work
Seeking a study place (§ 17(2))People looking for a university placeUp to 9 months€1,091 per monthAs required by the planned programmesLimited part-time work
Seeking a training place (§ 17(1))People under 35 looking for an AusbildungUp to 9 months€1,091 per monthB1 GermanCheck the current rules
Job search after study (§ 20(1) no. 1)Graduates of German universities, from inside GermanyUp to 18 monthsOwn meansNone setAny work allowed
Job search after researchResearchers after their project, from inside GermanyUp to 18 monthsOwn meansNone setCheck the current rules

Which one fits?

  • You have a degree or vocational qualification and want a job: the Opportunity Card.
  • You want to study but are not admitted yet: seeking a study place.
  • You want an Ausbildung, are under 35 and have B1: seeking a training place.
  • Not sure? Use the route finder.
