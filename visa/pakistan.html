Germany visa from Pakistan

Islamabad or Karachi, the online portal, appointments, documents and fraud warnings.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Which mission is responsible

Where you liveResponsible mission
Sindh, BalochistanConsulate General of the Federal Republic of Germany, Karachi
Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan, Azad Jammu and KashmirEmbassy of the Federal Republic of Germany, Islamabad

You apply at the mission responsible for the place where you live. The Honorary Consul in Lahore offers limited services, for example the signature attestation for opening a blocked account (€25 (about PKR 5,400) Verified); it does not issue visas.

WhatWhere

With a § 81a pre-approval (accelerated procedure)Priority appointment, as described on the missions’ work page

Categories move to the portal over time. Always open the missions’ page for your category first: long-term visas and short-term visas.

Fees and payment

  • National visa: €75 Verified; family reunion for children aged 0–17: €37.50 Verified.
  • Schengen visa: €90 Verified; children aged 6–12: €45 Verified.
  • Fees are paid in cash in Pakistani rupees at the mission. All figures and sources.

Documents, attestation and translations

  • Civil documents. The Embassy cannot attest or legalise Pakistani civil status documents. In family cases the mission may ask for document verification instead (fee stated as PKR 75,000 (PKR 79,000 from 1 Sep 2026) Needs review).
  • NADRA documents. Family cases usually need original NADRA birth and marriage registration certificates and the Nikah Nama with an English translation.
  • Educational documents. The missions make certified copies of educational documents in Islamabad (by online appointment) and Karachi (at fixed times). For this service no HEC cross-attestation is needed, and one set is free if you show that you plan to study. Universities and uni-assist set their own rules for copies; check before you order.
  • Translations. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan. Check which translations the mission and your university or authority accept.
  • Passport. Sign your passport. The missions do not issue a visa in an unsigned passport.
  • Board, IBCC, HEC and MOFA attestation. See the Pakistan document attestation guide, including what Germany actually asks for. An apostille from Pakistan has no effect in Germany.

Fraud warnings

Registration and appointments are free. The German Missions in Pakistan warn that anyone asking for money for a waiting-list registration or an appointment is a fraudster. Agents who offer paid “application checks” have no connection to the missions.

  • Pay visa fees only at the mission.
  • Check job and training offers directly with the company. Be wary of offers that ask you to pay for a contract.
  • Use only official websites ending in diplo.de for the portal and appointments.
  • Never share portal passwords or one-time codes with an agent.
  • Declare cash of €10,000 or more when you enter the EU.

Limits of jurisdiction

  • The mission can only accept applications from people who live in its district.
  • A mission in Pakistan cannot decide on matters that belong to the authorities in Germany, such as residence permits after arrival, recognition of qualifications or university admission.
  • Pages for Islamabad and Karachi sometimes differ (for example vocational training is still in person in Karachi). Follow the page for your mission.

History

Archived The Consulate General Karachi suspended services for non-EU citizens on 28 July 2025, and services later resumed. This is kept for context only; check the mission’s website for its current status.
