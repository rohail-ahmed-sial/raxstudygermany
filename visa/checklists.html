Visa document checklists

A checklist for each route, with statuses and notes, saved in your browser.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

Choose your route to see a starting checklist. Give each document a status and add notes. Everything is saved in this browser only; export or print it to keep a copy. The German mission’s own checklist for your category always comes first: add anything it asks for that is missing here.

Statuses: Not started, In progress, Ready for review, Completed, Needs official verification, Not applicable.

Student visa

§ 16b AufenthG. Read the guide.

Valid passportIssued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
Application form and declarationsThrough the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
Biometric photosRecent, white background, as specified by the mission.
Admission letter or conditional admissionFrom a state-recognised German university, Studienkolleg or preparatory course.
Previous degree certificates and transcriptsSchool certificates (Matric, Intermediate) and any university degrees: mandatory from the start of the online application.
Proof of tuition payment, if your programme charges feesMandatory if applicable.
Proof of financingBlocked account confirmation (€992 per month in 2026), formal obligation letter (Verpflichtungserklärung) or HEC scholarship.
Language certificateGerman or English, as required by the programme.
Health insuranceTravel or incoming insurance until you enrol; then statutory or private student insurance in Germany.
CV and motivation letter, if requested
Translations and certified copies where requiredCheck which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
Visa feePaid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
Additional documents specified by the responsible German missionAlways download the latest checklist for your category and mission.
