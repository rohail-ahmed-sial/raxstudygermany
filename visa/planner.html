Visa application planner

Your own timeline for each step, saved in your browser. Nothing is submitted.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

Plan your own dates for each step of your application. The planner is a preparation tool: it does not submit anything, book appointments or contact any authority. Processing times vary, so it never adds times for you. Your plan is saved in this browser only, with no account.

    Need the documents for your route? Use the checklists. Not sure about the route? Try the route finder.
