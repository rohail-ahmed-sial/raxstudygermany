Schengen visa for Germany from Pakistan

Visits of up to 90 days: appointments, fees, insurance and documents.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
EU Visa Code
For
Tourism, visiting family or friends, business trips and conferences of up to 90 days in any 180-day period.
Fee
€90 Verified; children aged 6–12: €45 Verified. Paid in PKR.
Processing
usually about 14 calendar days Verified.
Insurance
Travel health insurance with €30,000 minimum cover Verified.
When to apply
Up to six months before your trip.
Form
Videx online form, printed with the barcode.

How to get an appointment in Pakistan

  1. Register on the waiting listThe Embassy in Islamabad and the Consulate General in Karachi use a free online registration. You get one appointment per person.
  2. Wait for your invitationYou are contacted when an appointment is available. Anyone who asks you for money for a registration or an appointment is a fraudster.
  3. Fill in VidexComplete the Videx form and print it with the barcode.
  4. Attend the appointmentBring your passport, documents and the fee. Your fingerprints are taken.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Documents

The documents depend on the purpose of your trip; the missions publish a checklist for each one.

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Travel itinerary and flight reservation
  • Accommodation evidence
    Hotel bookings or invitation from your host.
  • Proof of funds
    Bank statements as required by the mission.
  • Travel health insurance (minimum €30,000)
    Valid for all Schengen states for the whole trip, including repatriation.
  • Purpose documents
    Invitation letter, business letter, conference registration.
  • Proof of ties to Pakistan
    Employment letter, studies, family, property.
  • Videx form with barcode
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

If your visa is refused

The remonstration procedure (asking the mission to review a refusal) was abolished from 1 July 2025. If you are refused, the refusal notice explains your options, such as applying again with better documents or taking legal action.

Common mistakes

  • Insurance that does not cover all Schengen states or the whole trip.
  • No clear proof of ties to Pakistan (job, studies, family).
  • Paying agents for waiting-list registration.
  • Travelling with €10,000 or more in cash without declaring it.

What this visa does not allow

  • Working in Germany (some business activities are allowed).
  • Staying longer than 90 days in 180 days.
  • Studying or training long-term, or changing to a residence permit in Germany in normal cases.
