Recognition and recognition partnership

Close the gap to full recognition in Germany, with a job or a training measure.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 16d AufenthG
For
People whose qualification needs further training or a practical phase in Germany before full recognition.
Language
Recognition partnership: German A2. Recognition measures: usually A2, sometimes more.
Money
Company-based measure: €1,200 gross per month Verified. School-based measure: €1,091 per month Verified.
Apply in Pakistan
Online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt (recognition is listed on the portal).
Visa fee
€75 Verified

Do you need recognition?

  • Regulated professions (for example doctors, nurses and teachers): you need recognition or a licence before you can work in the profession.
  • Non-regulated professions: recognition is not always required to work, but the visa for qualified professionals needs it. The experienced-worker route does not.
  • University degrees: check anabin or apply for a Statement of Comparability from the ZAB.

The official Recognition in Germany portal has a Recognition Finder that tells you which authority is responsible.

Recognition measures

If the authority finds differences between your qualification and the German one, you get a notice listing them (a deficit notice). You can then come to Germany for an adaptation course, training or a practical phase to close the gap.

Recognition partnership

  • A qualification of at least two years’ training, or a degree, recognised by the state where you got it (evidence from anabin or the ZAB).
  • A qualified job offer from an employer suited to train you.
  • A written agreement in which you and the employer commit to completing the recognition procedure.
  • German at A2 and approval by the Federal Employment Agency.
  • Issued for up to 12 months, and can be extended up to three years in total.

Documents

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Recognition notice (Defizitbescheid) or evaluation
    From the competent authority, ZAB or anabin.
  • Job offer and recognition partnership agreement, or place in a recognition measure
  • German A2 certificate
  • Proof of financing or salary
    Company-based measure: €1,200 gross per month (2026); school-based: €1,091 per month.
  • Declaration of employment
    If you will be employed.
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

How to apply from Pakistan

Apply online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt. Keep translations of your qualification documents ready; the recognition authority sets its own translation rules.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Common mistakes

  • Starting the visa before the recognition procedure or partnership agreement is in place.
  • A2 certificate missing or too old for the employer.
