Visa for qualified professionals

For people with a recognised qualification and a qualified job offer.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 18a AufenthG (vocational qualification), § 18b (academic qualification)
For
People with a qualification recognised in Germany (or a degree comparable to a German one) and a qualified job offer.
Approval
Usually the Federal Employment Agency.
Age 45 or older
Salary of at least €55,770 per year Verified, or proof of adequate pension provision, when applying for the first time.
Length
Up to four years, or the length of the contract if shorter.
Apply in Pakistan
Online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt.
Visa fee
€75 Verified

Requirements

  • Recognition. A vocational qualification must be recognised as equivalent by the competent German authority. A university degree must be recognised or comparable to a German degree (check anabin, or get a Statement of Comparability from the ZAB).
  • A concrete job offer for qualified work. In general the job no longer has to match your qualification exactly, except in regulated professions.
  • Approval by the Federal Employment Agency, if required for your job.
  • A licence to practise if your profession is regulated (for example medicine or nursing).
  • Age 45+: the salary threshold of €55,770 per year or pension proof.

Documents

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Employment contract or binding job offer
  • Recognition notice or Statement of Comparability / anabin printout
  • Declaration of employment (Erklärung zum Beschäftigungsverhältnis)
    Completed by the employer.
  • Licence to practise (regulated professions only)
  • CV and work references
  • Pension proof or salary evidence if you are 45+
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

How to apply from Pakistan

  1. Start recognitionUse the Recognition Finder on the official portal to find the authority responsible for your profession. Recognition can take months, so start early.
  2. Find an employerThe employer completes the declaration of employment. Ask whether they will use the accelerated procedure (§ 81a).
  3. Apply onlineUpload your application on digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt. With a § 81a pre-approval you can get a priority appointment.
  4. Appointment and decisionAttend the appointment with your originals and pay the fee in PKR.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Common mistakes

  • Applying before recognition is complete.
  • Assuming any degree is “comparable”: the university and the degree must both be listed appropriately.
  • Not checking the 45+ salary rule.
  • Fake job offers: confirm the company exists and that you speak to it directly.

After arrival

Register your address and apply for your residence permit. Skilled workers can apply for a settlement permit after three years, if they have paid 36 months of pension contributions, have German at B1 and meet the other conditions.

What this route does not allow

  • Working in a regulated profession without the licence.
  • Changing to a job that is not qualified employment.
