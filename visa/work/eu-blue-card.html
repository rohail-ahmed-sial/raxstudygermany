EU Blue Card Germany 2026

For university graduates with a qualifying job: 2026 salary thresholds, recognition and the application.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 18g AufenthG
For
University graduates with a qualifying job offer of at least six months.
Salary (2026)
At least €50,700 per year Verified. Lower threshold of €45,934.20 per year Verified for shortage occupations, career starters (degree within the last three years) and IT specialists without a degree.
German
Not required. German speeds up permanent residence.
Permanent residence
Settlement permit after 27 months of qualified work with German at A1, or after 21 months with German at B1.
Apply in Pakistan
Online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt.
Visa fee
€75 Verified

Requirements

  • A German university degree, or a foreign degree that is recognised or comparable (check that both your university and your degree are listed in anabin, or get a Statement of Comparability from the ZAB).
  • A job offer or contract for at least six months that matches your qualification.
  • The salary threshold for 2026: €50,700 per year, or €45,934.20 per year in the cases listed above.
  • IT specialists without a degree: at least three years of relevant professional experience in the last seven years.
  • A licence to practise if the profession is regulated.

Shortage occupations include, among others, scientists, mathematicians and engineers, doctors and nursing professionals, teachers, ICT professionals and some managers. The official list is on the Make it in Germany EU Blue Card page.

Old figures still online. The Consulate General Karachi’s Blue Card page still shows the thresholds “as per 2025” (€48,300 / €43,759.80 Outdated). The 2026 thresholds are higher. Use the current figures.

Documents

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Employment contract
    At least six months, matching your qualification.
  • Degree certificate and recognition evidence
    anabin H+ printout or Statement of Comparability.
  • Declaration of employment
    Completed by the employer.
  • For IT specialists without a degree: proof of 3 years of IT experience in the last 7
  • CV
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

How to apply from Pakistan

  1. Check your degreeanabin: university marked H+ and degree listed as equivalent, or a Statement of Comparability from the ZAB.
  2. Get the job offerThe employer completes the declaration of employment. The accelerated procedure (§ 81a) can shorten the process.
  3. Apply onlineUpload your application on digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt and attend your appointment.
  4. After arrivalRegister your address; the foreigners’ authority issues the EU Blue Card.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Family

Spouses of EU Blue Card holders do not need to prove German before arrival, and the living-space rule has exceptions for Blue Card holders. See family reunification.

Common mistakes

  • Using salary thresholds from a previous year.
  • A contract shorter than six months.
  • A degree from an institution not classified as H+ in anabin, without a Statement of Comparability.

What this route does not allow

  • Working in a job that does not match your qualification.
  • Falling below the salary threshold.
