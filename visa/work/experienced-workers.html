Visa for experienced workers

Work in Germany with experience and a home-country qualification, without German recognition.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 19c(2) AufenthG
For
People with professional experience whose qualification is not (yet) recognised in Germany.
Qualification
Recognised by the state where you obtained it: a degree or at least two years of vocational training.
Experience
At least two years in the last five years, related to the job.
Salary (2026)
At least €45,630 per year Verified, or pay under a collective agreement. Aged 45+: €55,770 per year Verified or pension proof.
German
Not required by the rule.
Visa fee
€75 Verified

Requirements

  • A qualification that is recognised by the state in the country where you got it (you can show this with an anabin printout or a ZAB evaluation).
  • Two years of professional experience in the last five years.
  • A job offer in a non-regulated profession (this route does not work for regulated professions such as nursing or medicine).
  • The salary threshold, or an employer bound by a collective agreement.
  • Approval by the Federal Employment Agency.

Documents

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Employment contract
    Non-regulated profession.
  • Qualification certificate
    State-recognised in the country of issue; ZAB or anabin evaluation.
  • Proof of two years’ experience in the last five
    Work certificates, contracts.
  • Declaration of employment
  • Salary evidence or collective agreement
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

How to apply from Pakistan

Apply online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt if your category is listed there; otherwise follow the mission’s instructions. Gather work certificates that show your tasks and dates clearly.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Common mistakes

  • Experience that is older than five years, or not related to the job.
  • A training qualification that is not state-recognised in Pakistan.
  • Using this route for a regulated profession.

What this route does not allow

  • Work in a regulated profession.
  • Work below the salary threshold or outside a collective agreement.
