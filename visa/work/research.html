Research visa Germany

For researchers with a hosting agreement at a recognised institution.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 18d AufenthG
For
Researchers with a hosting agreement or contract with a recognised research institution.
Length
Issued for at least one year.
Money
The institution commits to cover your costs, unless the post is mainly publicly funded. Self-financed: €1,091 per month Verified.
Apply in Pakistan
Not on the online portal: register for a regular appointment.
Visa fee
€75 Verified

Requirements

  • A hosting agreement or employment contract with a research institution recognised for this procedure.
  • A PhD, or a degree that gives access to doctoral programmes.
  • The institution’s written commitment to cover your living and return costs (where required).
  • Health insurance.

The research page of the German Missions in Pakistan lists €1,027 per month for living costs Needs review, which is lower than the 2026 federal amount for self-financed research (€1,091 per month). Check which applies to you.

Documents

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Hosting agreement or employment contract
    With a recognised research institution.
  • Cost commitment from the institution
    Unless the position is mainly publicly funded.
  • Degree certificates
    PhD or degree giving access to PhD programmes.
  • Proof of financing
    Scholarship or salary; self-financed: €1,091 per month (2026).
  • Health insurance
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

After your research

After your research you can stay up to 18 months to look for a job that matches your qualification. A settlement permit is possible later if you meet the conditions; see the official settlement permit page.
