Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)

Look for a job in Germany for up to a year: the points table, money and the application.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

This is a plain-language summary to help you prepare. The German mission responsible for you decides what it needs, and its latest checklist always comes first.

Legal basis
§ 20a AufenthG (Chancenkarte)
For
Skilled people who want to look for a job in Germany.
Length
Up to one year. Can be extended up to two years in specific cases, for example with a job offer.
Work allowed
Part-time up to 20 hours a week, and trial work of up to two weeks per employer.
Money to show
€1,091 per month Verified (2026)
Language
Points route: German A1 or English B2 at least.
Apply in Pakistan
Online through digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt.
Visa fee
€75 Verified

Two ways to qualify

  • Full recognition. If your qualification is fully recognised in Germany (or you have a German degree), you do not need points.
  • Points route. You need a qualification recognised by the state where you got it (a degree or at least two years of vocational training), German A1 or English B2, and at least 6 points.

Money

For 2026 the federal amount is €1,091 per month Verified for the planned stay, usually shown with a blocked account or a formal obligation letter. The Consulate General Karachi’s Opportunity Card page lists €1,027 per month Needs review per month and €500 Needs review for a return ticket. Because these differ, check the current checklist of your mission before you pay into a blocked account.

Documents

  • Valid passport
    Issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 3 months after the planned departure, with two blank pages, and signed by you (the Embassy cannot print a visa in an unsigned passport).
  • Application form and declarations
    Through the Consular Services Portal where your category is online, otherwise the Videx form or the mission’s form.
  • Biometric photos
    Recent, white background, as specified by the mission.
  • Qualification certificates
    Degree or at least two years’ vocational training, state-recognised in the issuing country.
  • Recognition evidence
    Full recognition, or anabin / ZAB evaluation for the points route.
  • Language certificate
    German A1 or English B2 at least; higher German levels for points.
  • Proof of financing
    €1,091 per month (2026) for the planned stay.
  • Evidence for each point you claim
    Work experience certificates, age, previous stays, partner’s qualifications.
  • Health insurance
  • CV
  • Translations and certified copies where required
    Check which documents need a translation. There are no sworn translators in Pakistan; ask the mission what it accepts.
  • Visa fee
    Paid at the mission in PKR at the current exchange rate.
  • Additional documents specified by the responsible German mission
    Always download the latest checklist for your category and mission.

How to apply from Pakistan

  1. Check your qualificationanabin or a ZAB evaluation shows that your degree or training is state-recognised. For partial recognition, apply to the competent authority.
  2. Collect evidence for every pointLanguage certificates, work certificates, proof of earlier stays.
  3. Arrange money and insuranceBlocked account or formal obligation letter, and health insurance.
  4. Apply onlineThrough digital.diplo.de/visa-ewt, then attend your appointment and pay the fee in PKR.

Which mission? If you live in Sindh or Balochistan, the Consulate General in Karachi is responsible. For all other regions (Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir) the Embassy in Islamabad is responsible. More for applicants in Pakistan.

Common mistakes

  • Counting points you cannot prove.
  • A qualification shorter than two years of training.
  • Planning to work full-time on the card: only part-time work and trial work are allowed.

After you find a job

When you find qualified work, you can apply in Germany for a residence permit for that job (for example as a skilled worker or EU Blue Card holder) if you meet the conditions.

What this route does not allow

  • Full-time work.
  • Self-employment as the main purpose.
  • Bringing family members automatically.
