Overall band rounding

Average the four section bands. If the average ends in .25 it rounds up to the next half band; if it ends in .75 it rounds up to the next whole band; otherwise it rounds down. 6.25 → 6.5, 6.75 → 7.0, 6.1 → 6.0, 6.6 → 6.5.
