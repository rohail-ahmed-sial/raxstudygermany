IELTS Academic Reading practice

Six original passages, 80 questions, every main question type.

Original academic passages with the main IELTS question types. Spend about 20 minutes per passage. Answer only from the passage, not from your own knowledge.

Question typeHow to approach it
True / False / Not given“False” means the passage says the opposite; “Not given” means the passage does not say. Do not guess from general knowledge.
Matching headingsRead the first and last sentences of each paragraph. Choose the heading that sums up the whole paragraph, not one detail.
Sentence and summary completionKeep to the word limit and copy the words exactly from the passage.
Multiple choiceUnderline key words in the question, find the matching part of the passage and eliminate wrong options.
