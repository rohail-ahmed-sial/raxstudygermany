Sources

Official sources for this page

  • IBCC: Attestation FAQs Verified
    Original certificate/DMC fee PKR 1,200 each; one-time fee; appointment documents returned the same day; courier processed within 10 working days and only at the Islamabad Secretariat and Karachi office; blood relatives may submit; lifetime QR code; IBCC does not correct name or date-of-birth errors. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Express service FAQs Verified
    Express attestation PKR 3,000 per original and PKR 1,500 per photocopy; documents returned within about an hour; evenings 6 to 11 pm in summer and 5 to 9 pm in winter; this FAQ says Islamabad Secretariat only. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Submission modes for attestation Verified
    Appointment at Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Bahawalpur, Quetta or Peshawar; Express evening service 5 to 9 pm in Islamabad and Lahore; courier through Pakistan Post, Leopards, M&P or TCS, returned within 15 working days. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Attestation application process Verified
    Register and log in at attest.ibcc.edu.pk, enter details, book Appointment (morning), Express (evening, Islamabad and Lahore) or Courier, pay through 1Bill or a UBL challan, and submit. Lists the six IBCC offices. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Verification of attested documents Verified
    QR-coded attestations can be verified online free of charge. Manual verification of older documents (for organisations) costs PKR 600 per certificate plus PKR 300 courier charges. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • HEC: Degree attestation Verified
    Fully online, paperless attestation through eservices.hec.gov.pk, open 24/7. PKR 3,000 per document (degree, transcript, provisional certificate or HEC equivalence letter) through 1-Link 1Bill. No office visit or originals. Photocopy attestation no longer offered. Four stages ending with a downloadable e-Attestation Certificate. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents Verified
    Prior verification authority for each document type, documents not attested, general attestation free (revenue stamps only), apostille fees from 8 July 2024, prior online registration for apostille from 27 July 2026, offices and courier companies. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • MOFA: Standard Operating Procedures for attestation of documents (May 2026, PDF) Verified
    General attestation free except revenue stamps; apostille PKR 3,000 personal and educational, PKR 4,500 legal, PKR 12,000 commercial; most documents processed within two hours; camp office attestation equal to the Ministry; office addresses. Parts of the PDF are scanned text with errors. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026) Needs review
    MOFA circular of 14 October 2026: Digital Attestation System effective 12 October 2026 at headquarters and liaison offices in Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta and Gujrat; e-Certificates by email after the process and fee; no visit needed except where physical attendance is necessary. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • German Missions in Pakistan: Legal Verified
    Pakistani documents cannot be attested or legalised by the German Embassy. Certified copies of educational documents at Islamabad (online booking) and Karachi (Monday to Thursday, 1:30 to 2:30 pm); no HEC cross-attestation needed; up to 4 sets per day; one set free with proof of intent to study. Page dated 14 April 2026. Last checked 29 October 2026.

Verified: we read the official page on the date shown. Needs review: the page could not be read automatically, was unclear or conflicted with another official page, or the information comes from press reports. Outdated: replaced by a newer official instruction.
