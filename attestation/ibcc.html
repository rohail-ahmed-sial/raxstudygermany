IBCC attestation: SSC, HSSC and board documents

Board verification, the IBCC portal, fees, submission modes and what comes next.

Last checked: 29 October 2026. Fees and steps carry their own status.

The Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) attests school-level qualifications issued in Pakistan: SSC and HSSC certificates, marks sheets and diplomas of boards (BISEs and BTEs), school leaving certificates up to class 8, certain madrassah certificates and IBCC equivalence certificates. It does not attest university degrees (HEC does), private institutions’ documents, provisional, migration or character certificates, or damaged documents.

Portal
attest.ibcc.edu.pk
Fee
PKR 1,200 per original document; Express PKR 3,000 per original document and PKR 1,500 per photocopy
Offices
Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Bahawalpur. Addresses
Who may submit
You, or a blood relative who can prove the relationship
Verification
Each attestation has a lifetime QR code that can be checked online for free

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Identify your document

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    IBCC attests school-level qualifications issued in Pakistan. University documents go to HEC, and civil documents go to NADRA or the Union Council and then MOFA.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Your Matric/SSC and Intermediate/HSSC certificates (sanad) and marks sheets (DMC/result card)
    • Any diploma issued by a BISE or BTE
    • IBCC equivalence certificate if you studied O/A levels or abroad

    What to do

    1. Check that the document is one IBCC attests: SSC/HSSC certificates, marks sheets and diplomas of BISEs and BTEs, school leaving certificates up to class 8 (countersigned by the CEO/EDO of the Directorate of Education), madrassah Shahadat ul Sanwia Aama and Khasa, and IBCC equivalence certificates.
    2. O/A levels and other foreign school qualifications: IBCC attests them only together with an IBCC equivalence certificate. Apply for equivalence first, or at the same time.

    Official links

    • Requirements for attestation

    Common mistakes

    • Sending a university degree to IBCC. Degrees and transcripts are attested by HEC.
    • Applying for attestation of O/A level results without the equivalence certificate.

    Sources: IBCC: Requirements for attestation, IBCC: Attestation FAQs

  2. Step 2

    Get your board verification

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    Your board must verify the certificate or marks sheet first, and the verification must show online in the IBCC Attestation Portal before you apply.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Originals and photocopies of the documents to verify
    • Copy of your CNIC (or B-form)
    • The board’s verification fee (paid at the board’s bank or online)

    What to do

    1. Check that your name, father’s name and date of birth match your CNIC exactly. IBCC does not correct errors: the board must correct and re-verify them first.
    2. Open your board’s website and check the current verification fee, the bank or online payment method and whether the board is connected to the IBCC portal.
    3. Pay the fee, request verification for IBCC, and keep the deposit slip.
    4. Wait until the verification appears in the IBCC Attestation Portal. If it does not appear after a few days, contact your board before you apply to IBCC.
    5. Duplicate or revised certificate: get it and its verification from the board, then apply normally.
    6. Madrassah certificates: the wafaq, tanzeem or madrassah sends the verification in a sealed envelope. An open envelope is not accepted.

    Official links

    • Requirements for attestation
    • Attestation FAQs

    Common mistakes

    • Applying to IBCC before the board verification shows in the portal.
    • A spelling difference between the certificate and the CNIC.
    • Using another board’s fee: each board sets its own.

    Applicant notes, not official: some boards asked for passport photos, several copies and a sealed envelope to carry to IBCC. Since April 2024 boards verify records online in the IBCC portal, so this applies only if your board tells you to. Board fees in the notes (PKR 1,100 to 6,000) were not confirmed.

    Sources: IBCC: Requirements for attestation, IBCC: Attestation FAQs, Dawn: education boards connected to the IBCC Attestation Portal (April 2024)

  3. Step 3

    Apply on the IBCC Attestation Portal

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    The application is made online. You choose how you will submit the documents and pay the fee there.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Your own email address and mobile number
    • Board name, year, roll number and the documents to attest
    • Decide how many attested photocopies you need

    What to do

    1. Register and log in at attest.ibcc.edu.pk with your own email. Do not let an agent create the account.
    2. Enter personal details exactly as on your CNIC, then your education details.
    3. Select the documents (originals) and the number of photocopies you want attested. Many universities and embassies keep an attested copy.
    4. Choose the submission mode: Appointment (morning service), Express (evening service) or Courier. For Appointment and Express, pick the centre, date and time.
    5. Download and print the application form. If you change the application later, download it again.

    Official links

    • IBCC Attestation Portal
    • Attestation application process

    Common mistakes

    • Typing details differently from the CNIC.
    • Printing an old version of the form after editing the application.

    Applicant notes, not official: if a relative will go to the centre, select that person in the application, because only that person is let in. Not stated on the IBCC pages we read.

    Sources: IBCC: Attestation application process, IBCC Attestation Portal

  4. Step 4

    Pay the fee

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    Pay the amount the portal calculates. The fee is paid once; if documents are returned for a deficiency, you do not pay again after fixing it.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Mobile banking app, Easypaisa or JazzCash (1Bill), or a printed challan for UBL

    What to do

    1. Pay through 1Bill using the consumer number, or download the challan and pay at a UBL branch.
    2. Check that the payment shows as paid in the portal. Keep the receipt or challan.

    Official links

    • IBCC Attestation Portal

    Common mistakes

    • Paying at an unofficial shop that keeps your account details.
    • Losing the paid challan: you need it at submission.

    Sources: IBCC: Attestation application process, IBCC: Attestation FAQs, IBCC: Express service FAQs

  5. Step 5

    Prepare your documents

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    Bring or send exactly what IBCC lists. Some items apply only to certain documents or applicants.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • For every SSC/HSSC application: printed application form; the original certificate or marks sheet plus one copy for office record; the paid challan or online receipt printout; photocopies of the student’s and father’s CNIC
    • Only if you want attested photocopies: the photocopies to attest
    • Only if your board is connected online: a printout of the online verification (IBCC FAQ)
    • Only if a relative submits: proof of the blood relationship
    • School leaving certificates only: last annual progress report signed by the principal, B-form and father’s CNIC copy
    • Madrassah certificates only: the sealed verification envelope

    What to do

    1. Put the documents in the order of the application form and check the copies against the originals.

    Official links

    • Requirements for attestation

    Common mistakes

    • Bringing originals without copies for the office record.
    • Sending someone who is not a blood relative.

    Sources: IBCC: Requirements for attestation, IBCC: Attestation FAQs, IBCC: Attestation application process

  6. Step 6

    Submit and collect

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    How you submit depends on the mode you booked. Attested documents carry a lifetime QR code that anyone can verify online for free.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Everything from the previous step
    • Your original CNIC

    What to do

    1. Appointment: go to the centre at the booked time. Staff tell you whether to collect the same day or the next day.
    2. Express: go to the office in the evening. Documents are usually returned within about an hour. IBCC’s pages differ on where Express is offered (Islamabad, and Lahore according to the submission-modes page).
    3. Courier: send the application and documents through Pakistan Post, Leopards, M&P or TCS to the IBCC office named in your application. Returns take 10 to 15 working days and go to addresses in Pakistan only. Courier attestation is handled by the Islamabad Secretariat and the Karachi office.
    4. Check every stamp and the QR code before you leave or when the courier arrives.

    Official links

    • Submission modes
    • Express service FAQs

    Common mistakes

    • Missing your appointment slot.
    • Choosing courier with a close deadline.
    • Sending several applicants in one courier envelope: each needs its own envelope and application ID.

    Applicant notes, not official: morning slots (8:30 to 12:30) are easier to collect the same day; centres in Lahore and Islamabad are busy, so applicants from south Punjab often choose Bahawalpur. Practical advice, not official.

    Sources: IBCC: Attestation application process, IBCC: Submission modes for attestation, IBCC: Attestation FAQs, IBCC: Express service FAQs

  7. Step 7

    Next step after IBCC

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    For use abroad, IBCC-attested documents usually go to MOFA next. Which MOFA service you need depends on the destination and the receiving institution.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Your IBCC-attested originals

    What to do

    1. Ask the receiving university, employer or embassy exactly what it needs: MOFA general attestation, an apostille, certified copies or nothing more.
    2. For Germany, read the Germany section: an apostille from Pakistan is not accepted by Germany.

    Official links

    • MOFA attestation and apostille
    • Using documents in Germany

    Common mistakes

    • Assuming IBCC attestation alone guarantees acceptance abroad.

    Sources: MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents, HCCH: Apostille Convention status table

Sources

Official sources for this page

  • IBCC: Requirements for attestation Verified
    Document lists for school leaving certificates (up to class 8), SSC/HSSC/diplomas of BISEs and BTEs, and madrassah certificates. Board verification must be available online in the IBCC Attestation Portal before submission. Only blood relatives may apply for the applicant. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Attestation application process Verified
    Register and log in at attest.ibcc.edu.pk, enter details, book Appointment (morning), Express (evening, Islamabad and Lahore) or Courier, pay through 1Bill or a UBL challan, and submit. Lists the six IBCC offices. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Submission modes for attestation Verified
    Appointment at Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Bahawalpur, Quetta or Peshawar; Express evening service 5 to 9 pm in Islamabad and Lahore; courier through Pakistan Post, Leopards, M&P or TCS, returned within 15 working days. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Attestation FAQs Verified
    Original certificate/DMC fee PKR 1,200 each; one-time fee; appointment documents returned the same day; courier processed within 10 working days and only at the Islamabad Secretariat and Karachi office; blood relatives may submit; lifetime QR code; IBCC does not correct name or date-of-birth errors. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Express service FAQs Verified
    Express attestation PKR 3,000 per original and PKR 1,500 per photocopy; documents returned within about an hour; evenings 6 to 11 pm in summer and 5 to 9 pm in winter; this FAQ says Islamabad Secretariat only. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Verification of attested documents Verified
    QR-coded attestations can be verified online free of charge. Manual verification of older documents (for organisations) costs PKR 600 per certificate plus PKR 300 courier charges. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC Attestation Portal Needs review
    The official online application portal. We did not open the portal itself; its screens may differ from the descriptions here. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Dawn: education boards connected to the IBCC Attestation Portal (April 2024) Needs review
    IBCC press release reported by Dawn: from 3 April 2024 several boards verify SSC/HSSC records online, so students no longer bring sealed verification envelopes. Last checked 29 October 2026.
