Apostille from Pakistan: when it works

When an apostille helps, the eight countries where it does not, and how to apply.

Last checked: 29 October 2026. Fees and steps carry their own status.

An apostille is a certificate under the Hague Apostille Convention of 1961. Between countries where the Convention applies, it replaces the chain of legalisation by embassies. Pakistan acceded on 8 July 2022 and the Convention entered into force for Pakistan on 9 March 2023. MOFA issues apostilles for eligible public documents.

Where a Pakistani apostille does not work

These states objected to Pakistan’s accession, so the Convention does not apply between them and Pakistan. An apostille from Pakistan has no effect there:

Germany24 October 2022
Czech Republic23 November 2022
Poland30 November 2022
Austria12 December 2022

Netherlands15 December 2022
Denmark2 January 2023
Greece5 January 2023

From the HCCH status table, checked 29 October 2026. Many Gulf countries are not parties to the Convention at all. Always check the status table and the receiving institution’s instructions.

For Germany: do not order an apostille. Follow what your university, uni-assist or the German mission asks for. Using documents in Germany.

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Check that an apostille will be accepted

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    An apostille only works between Pakistan and a country where the Apostille Convention applies with Pakistan.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • The receiving institution’s written instructions

    What to do

    1. Check the HCCH status table for the destination country.
    2. Germany, the Czech Republic, Poland, Austria, Finland, the Netherlands, Denmark and Greece objected to Pakistan’s accession. An apostille from Pakistan has no effect in these countries.

    Official links

    • HCCH status table

    Common mistakes

    • Assuming every European country accepts a Pakistani apostille.

    Sources: HCCH: Apostille Convention status table

  2. Step 2

    Complete prior verification

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    The same prior verification applies as for general attestation: IBCC for school documents, HEC for university documents, NADRA for NADRA documents.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Verified originals

    What to do

    1. Finish IBCC, HEC or NADRA steps first.

    Official links

    • Prior verification table

    Common mistakes

    • Applying for apostille before HEC or IBCC attestation.

    Sources: MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents

  3. Step 3

    Register and apply online

    Needs review Checked 29 October 2026

    From 27 July 2026 apostille applications must be registered online first; applications not submitted online are not processed. Since 12 October 2026 the Digital Attestation System also covers apostille.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Your own email address
    • Scans of your CNIC (both sides) and documents

    What to do

    1. Create an account, attach your CNIC, create an application, attach the documents and submit.
    2. Wait for approval and the challan by email.

    Official links

    • MOFA Apostille portal
    • MOFA Attestation portal

    Common mistakes

    • Going to an office without registering online first.

    Applicant notes, not official: MOFA’s notice of 5 March 2026 suspended the apostille portal for cybersecurity reasons; the later 27 July 2026 notice made online registration mandatory. Check that the portal is open.

    Sources: MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents, MOFA Apostille portal, Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026)

  4. Step 4

    Pay the apostille fee

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    PKR 3,000 per personal or educational document, PKR 4,500 per legal document and PKR 12,000 per commercial document (from 8 July 2024). Courier charges are extra.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Online banking, Easypaisa or a bank account

    What to do

    1. Pay the emailed challan by one of the methods MOFA lists.

    Official links

    • MOFA consular services

    Common mistakes

    • Paying before the application is approved.

    Sources: MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents, MOFA: Standard Operating Procedures for attestation of documents (May 2026, PDF)

  5. Step 5

    Submit originals as instructed and receive the apostille

    Needs review Checked 29 October 2026

    Educational documents go through the online appointment or an authorised courier; walk-in is for legal documents only. Under the digital system, e-Certificates are emailed.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Original CNIC and a photocopy
    • Original documents and a photocopy of each
    • Appointment slip if you have one

    What to do

    1. Visit the office you selected only after approval, or send the documents with an authorised courier (Gerry’s, Leopards, ECS, M&P or TCS).
    2. Check the apostille certificate or e-Certificate before you leave or when it arrives.

    Official links

    • MOFA consular services

    Common mistakes

    • Using courier for powers of attorney, divorce, commercial documents or affidavits: these need physical presence.

    Applicant notes, not official: MOFA Lahore once accepted courier only, and same-day processing took 1 to 2 hours. Older observations; not confirmed for October 2026.

    Sources: MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents, MOFA: Standard Operating Procedures for attestation of documents (May 2026, PDF), Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026)
