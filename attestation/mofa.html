MOFA attestation and apostille

General attestation and the apostille, the new digital workflow, and what to verify first.

Last checked: 29 October 2026. Fees and steps carry their own status.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attests Pakistani documents for use abroad after the responsible authority has verified them. It offers two different services: general attestation and the apostille. They are not interchangeable.

General attestationApostille
What it isMOFA confirms the document for use abroad. Some countries then also want their own embassy’s legalisation or verification.A certificate under the 1961 Apostille Convention, accepted without legalisation in countries where the Convention applies with Pakistan.
When to use itWhen the receiving institution asks for MOFA attestation, or the destination does not accept a Pakistani apostille.Only for a destination where the Convention applies with Pakistan, and only if the receiver asks for it. Not for Germany.
FeeNo MOFA fee (revenue stamps only)PKR 3,000 per document for personal and educational documents

Current MOFA digital workflow

Changed in October 2026. According to MOFA’s circular reported by the press, a Digital Attestation System took effect on 12 October 2026 at the Ministry and the liaison offices in Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta and Gujrat, with e-Certificates sent by email after processing and payment. Physical attendance is needed only in some cases. MOFA’s own portal pages could not be read automatically, so open How it works on the portal before you apply. Older instructions describe a counter or appointment process; follow the portal for your case.

  1. Step 1

    Get prior verification first

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    MOFA attests a document only after the authority that issued or verifies it has done so (see the table below).

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • IBCC-attested school documents, HEC e-Attestation for university documents, NADRA documents, or the verification listed for your document

    What to do

    1. Check the prior-verification table for each document.
    2. Documents not in Urdu or English need a translation with an English column on the same page.

    Official links

    • MOFA consular services

    Common mistakes

    • Bringing a degree to MOFA before HEC attestation.

    Sources: MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents, MOFA: Standard Operating Procedures for attestation of documents (May 2026, PDF)

  2. Step 2

    Choose General Attestation or Apostille

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    They are different services. Which one you need depends on the destination country and what the receiving institution asks for.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • The receiving institution’s written instructions

    What to do

    1. Apostille only helps in a country where the Apostille Convention applies with Pakistan. Germany and seven other countries objected to Pakistan’s accession, so an apostille from Pakistan has no effect there.
    2. General attestation confirms the document for use abroad; some countries then also want their embassy’s legalisation or verification.

    Official links

    • Apostille guide

    Common mistakes

    • Paying for an apostille for Germany.

    Sources: HCCH: Apostille Convention status table, MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents

  3. Step 3

    Register on the MOFA portal

    Needs review Checked 29 October 2026

    Since 12 October 2026 MOFA uses a Digital Attestation System for general attestation and apostille, according to its circular as reported by the press.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Your own email address
    • Your CNIC (front and back)

    What to do

    1. Open the MOFA attestation portal, create an account, verify your email and complete your profile.
    2. Enter your CNIC exactly as printed.

    Official links

    • MOFA Attestation portal
    • MOFA Digital Attestation portal

    Common mistakes

    • Using an agent’s email: all approvals arrive by email.

    Sources: Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026), MOFA Attestation portal, MOFA Digital Attestation portal

  4. Step 4

    Submit your documents online

    Needs review Checked 29 October 2026

    Select the service, enter the document details and upload clear scans. An officer reviews the submission.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Clear, complete scans of each document and its prior verification

    What to do

    1. Select General Attestation or Apostille, enter each document’s details, upload the scans, review and submit.
    2. Check your account and email for the officer’s review and instructions.

    Official links

    • MOFA Attestation portal

    Common mistakes

    • Blurred or partial scans.
    • Submitting documents that still need prior verification.

    Sources: Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026), MOFA Attestation portal

  5. Step 5

    Follow the instructions after review

    Needs review Checked 29 October 2026

    Depending on the service and your case, the portal asks you to pay, book an appointment or send or bring originals.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • The fee or revenue stamps, your original CNIC and the original documents if requested

    What to do

    1. General attestation has no MOFA fee; only revenue stamps of the prescribed value are pasted. Apostille costs PKR 3,000 per personal or educational document, with courier charges extra.
    2. Go to an office or send originals only if the portal tells you to. MOFA says physical attendance is needed only in some cases.

    Official links

    • MOFA consular services

    Common mistakes

    • Visiting an office without an approved application or appointment.

    Applicant notes, not official: Before October 2026: after approval you booked an appointment at a Foreign Affairs Liaison Office, the fee challan appeared after booking, and documents came back the same day from the delivery counter. This may still apply when physical attendance is required.

    Sources: MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents, MOFA: Standard Operating Procedures for attestation of documents (May 2026, PDF), Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026)

  6. Step 6

    Receive your attestation

    Needs review Checked 29 October 2026

    Under the digital system MOFA emails an e-Certificate once processing and payment are complete. Documents handled at a counter are returned with the stamp and signature.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Access to your email

    What to do

    1. Download and keep the e-Certificate as received. Check every stamp and signature on documents returned to you.

    Official links

    • MOFA Digital Attestation portal

    Common mistakes

    • Editing or re-saving the e-Certificate.

    Sources: Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026)

Prior verification by document type

MOFA attests a document only after this authority has verified it (from MOFA’s consular page).

DocumentVerify first with
Matric/SSC, Intermediate/HSSC certificates and marks sheets (BISE)Your board, then IBCC
School leaving certificatesIBCC, or the District Education Officer / PEIRA
University degrees and transcriptsHEC
Technical and vocational certificatesThe local technical board and NAVTTC
Madrassah certificates, Hifz-ul-QuranIBCC or HEC first
Foreign degrees and school qualificationsThe Pakistani mission in the issuing country, plus IBCC or HEC equivalence
ICAP / ICMAP results and certificatesICAP / ICMAP
ACCABritish Council and ACCA
NADRA documents (birth, family registration, marriage registration)Attested by MOFA directly
Nikah Nama / marriage registration certificateNikah Registrar’s signature and stamp, with the bride’s CNIC or NADRA FRC/CRC
Unmarried certificateUnion Council Secretary on official letterhead (or NADRA-issued)
Police character certificateDistrict Police Officer
Driving licenceOriginal plus NOC from the licensing authority

Not attested by MOFA

  • CNIC/NICOP and their translations
  • Domicile certificates
  • Photographs
  • Invitation letters and visa notes
  • Land registry and sale or purchase papers
  • Applications of any kind
  • Unmarried certificates on plain paper or letterhead in the name of a Chairman or Member

Offices and hours

The MOFA page says Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm; the May 2026 SOP says 8:00 am to 4:00 pm for Islamabad. Check before you go. All MOFA and IBCC offices.
