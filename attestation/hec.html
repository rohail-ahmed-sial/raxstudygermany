HEC attestation: degrees and transcripts

The fully online e-Attestation system for degrees and transcripts.

Last checked: 29 October 2026. Fees and steps carry their own status.

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) attests university-level documents. Since May 2026 attestation is fully online and paperless: you apply on the HEC e-Services portal, upload scans, pay online and download an e-Attestation Certificate. You do not visit HEC or send originals.

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Check that HEC attests your document

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    HEC attests university-level documents from HEC-recognised institutions. It does not attest Matric or Intermediate documents.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Degree, transcript (result card, result intimation card or detailed marks certificate), provisional certificate or HEC equivalence letter

    What to do

    1. Provisional certificates are accepted only if issued by the university’s examination office.
    2. Foreign degrees are not attested directly: only an HEC equivalence letter can be attested.
    3. MS/MPhil programmes launched after 7 November 2013 need an HEC NOC. PhD degrees must be in HEC’s PhD directory.
    4. Medical and nursing degrees need the PMDC/PNMC registration card or certificate, uploaded under Other Documents.
    5. HEC no longer attests photocopies. A transcript can be attested before the degree is issued if the university shows the academic status.

    Official links

    • HEC degree attestation

    Common mistakes

    • Sending Matric or Intermediate documents to HEC (they go to IBCC).
    • Documents with overwriting or alterations: they are not attested.

    Sources: HEC: Degree attestation

  2. Step 2

    Make your details match

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    HEC checks your personal details with NADRA. If your name, father’s name or date of birth differ between your CNIC and your documents, attestation will not start.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Your CNIC and every educational document from Matric onward

    What to do

    1. Compare spellings and dates on every document. Ask the issuing board or university to correct errors before you apply.

    Official links

    • HEC degree attestation

    Common mistakes

    • A one-letter difference between documents.

    Sources: HEC: Degree attestation

  3. Step 3

    Create your account and enter your education

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    Apply on the HEC e-Services portal, which is open 24 hours a day.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Your own email address and mobile number
    • Institution names, degree names, years and roll or registration numbers from Matric to your final degree

    What to do

    1. Sign up at eservices.hec.gov.pk with your own email and number, not an agent’s or a shop’s.
    2. Choose Degree Attestation, enter personal details, then education details from Matric to your final degree.
    3. Select only the degree you need attested, then the documents: degree, transcript, provisional certificate or equivalence letter.

    Official links

    • HEC e-Services portal

    Common mistakes

    • Selecting the wrong documents: HEC does not refund, adjust or transfer the fee for an incorrect selection.

    Sources: HEC: Degree attestation, HEC e-Services portal

  4. Step 4

    Upload clear scans

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    HEC works from your uploads; you do not send originals.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Colour scans of your CNIC (front and back) and of each document as clear, legible PDFs
    • A passport-size photo on a plain blue or white background

    What to do

    1. Use a scanner or a good scanning app. Crooked, blurred or cropped scans can send the application back.
    2. Upload supporting documents (registration cards, NOC) under Other Documents.

    Official links

    • HEC e-Services portal

    Common mistakes

    • Phone photos with shadows or cut edges.

    Sources: HEC: Degree attestation

  5. Step 5

    Pay the fee

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    PKR 3,000 per document (degree, transcript, provisional certificate or equivalence letter), not per page.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Online banking, JazzCash or Easypaisa (1-Link 1Bill)

    What to do

    1. Click Proceed for Payment to get a consumer number and pay through 1Bill.
    2. Then click Verify Payment. The application is submitted after the payment is verified.

    Official links

    • HEC e-Services portal

    Common mistakes

    • Forgetting to click Verify Payment.

    Sources: HEC: Degree attestation, Dawn: HEC launches online degree attestation system (11 May 2026)

  6. Step 6

    Scrutiny, university verification and final review

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    HEC checks your details with NADRA, sends deficiencies back to you as tasks, asks your university to verify your credentials and then does a final review.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Access to your HEC account, email and SMS

    What to do

    1. Check your account, email and SMS regularly and fix any deficiency task quickly.
    2. If your application waits a long time at the university stage, contact the university’s examination or registrar office.

    Official links

    • HEC degree attestation

    Common mistakes

    • Ignoring a deficiency task: the application stops until you respond.

    Sources: HEC: Degree attestation, Dawn: HEC launches online degree attestation system (11 May 2026)

  7. Step 7

    Download your e-Attestation Certificate

    Verified Checked 29 October 2026

    After approval HEC informs you by SMS and email, and you download the e-Attestation Certificate from your account.

    What to prepare, what to do, common mistakes

    What to prepare

    • Somewhere safe to keep the PDF

    What to do

    1. Download the certificate, keep the original PDF and print copies if needed.
    2. For use abroad, the next step is usually MOFA. Check what the receiving institution needs.

    Official links

    • MOFA attestation and apostille

    Common mistakes

    • Editing the PDF or sending a screenshot instead of the downloaded file.

    Sources: HEC: Degree attestation, Dawn: HEC launches online degree attestation system (11 May 2026)
