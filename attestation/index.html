Pakistan document attestation guide

Board, IBCC, HEC and MOFA explained step by step, with a route selector for your document and what Germany actually asks for.

Last checked: 29 October 2026. Fees and steps carry their own status.

Before Pakistani documents are used abroad, they usually have to be verified by the authority responsible for them. What you need depends on the type of document, who issued it, what it is for, the destination country and the institution that will receive it. This guide explains who does what, step by step, using the official IBCC, HEC and MOFA pages.

Before you start: ask the receiving university, employer or embassy for its written document requirements. Many people pay for steps they do not need, such as an apostille for Germany.

Step-by-step procedures

These are the usual sequences. Check the precise document category, purpose, destination and official instructions before you follow one.

  1. Matric / SSC and Intermediate / HSSC certificates and marks sheets

    1. Board verification
    2. IBCC attestation
    3. MOFA service, if the receiver requires it

  2. University degrees and transcripts

    1. HEC e-Attestation
    2. MOFA service, if the receiver requires it

  3. Technical or vocational certificates

    1. Issuing technical board
    2. NAVTTC
    3. MOFA service, if required

  4. Foreign-issued school or university qualifications

    1. Pakistani mission in the issuing country
    2. IBCC or HEC equivalence
    3. Further steps only if required

  5. Birth, family registration, Nikah Nama and other civil documents

    1. NADRA or Union Council certificate
    2. MOFA service, if required

Using Pakistani documents in Germany

  • No apostille for Germany. Germany objected to Pakistan’s accession to the Apostille Convention (24 October 2022), so an apostille from Pakistan has no effect in Germany. Verified
  • No legalisation by the German Embassy. The German Missions in Pakistan say Pakistani documents cannot be attested or legalised by the Embassy. Older guides that send you to the embassy for legalisation after MOFA are out of date for Germany.
  • Universities and uni-assist set the document rules. uni-assist’s Pakistan page asks for school leaving certificates with subjects and grades and for degree certificates “with the HEC attestation stamps”. Since May 2026 HEC issues an e-Attestation Certificate instead, so ask uni-assist or your university what they accept. Needs review
  • Certified copies. The German Embassy in Islamabad (online appointment) and the Consulate General in Karachi (Monday to Thursday, 1:30 to 2:30 pm) certify copies of educational documents. No HEC cross-attestation is needed for this service, up to 4 sets per person per day, and one set is free if you show you intend to study in Germany.
  • Visa applications. The student visa application on the Consular Services Portal asks for your certificates and transcripts as uploads. For family reunion the mission may ask for document verification. See the Pakistan visa page.

For Germany, the usual sensible order is: board and IBCC for school documents, HEC for university documents, then follow the exact instructions of your university, uni-assist or the German mission. Get MOFA attestation only if one of them asks for it.

Sources

Official sources for this page

  • IBCC: Requirements for attestation Verified
    Document lists for school leaving certificates (up to class 8), SSC/HSSC/diplomas of BISEs and BTEs, and madrassah certificates. Board verification must be available online in the IBCC Attestation Portal before submission. Only blood relatives may apply for the applicant. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • IBCC: Attestation application process Verified
    Register and log in at attest.ibcc.edu.pk, enter details, book Appointment (morning), Express (evening, Islamabad and Lahore) or Courier, pay through 1Bill or a UBL challan, and submit. Lists the six IBCC offices. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • HEC: Degree attestation Verified
    Fully online, paperless attestation through eservices.hec.gov.pk, open 24/7. PKR 3,000 per document (degree, transcript, provisional certificate or HEC equivalence letter) through 1-Link 1Bill. No office visit or originals. Photocopy attestation no longer offered. Four stages ending with a downloadable e-Attestation Certificate. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • MOFA: Consular services, attestation of documents Verified
    Prior verification authority for each document type, documents not attested, general attestation free (revenue stamps only), apostille fees from 8 July 2024, prior online registration for apostille from 27 July 2026, offices and courier companies. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Business Recorder: MOFA introduces digital attestation system (October 2026) Needs review
    MOFA circular of 14 October 2026: Digital Attestation System effective 12 October 2026 at headquarters and liaison offices in Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta and Gujrat; e-Certificates by email after the process and fee; no visit needed except where physical attendance is necessary. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • HCCH: Apostille Convention status table Verified
    Pakistan acceded on 8 July 2022; the Convention entered into force for Pakistan on 9 March 2023. Germany, the Czech Republic, Poland, Austria, Finland, the Netherlands, Denmark and Greece objected to Pakistan’s accession. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • German Missions in Pakistan: Legal Verified
    Pakistani documents cannot be attested or legalised by the German Embassy. Certified copies of educational documents at Islamabad (online booking) and Karachi (Monday to Thursday, 1:30 to 2:30 pm); no HEC cross-attestation needed; up to 4 sets per day; one set free with proof of intent to study. Page dated 14 April 2026. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • uni-assist: Pakistan country information Needs review
    Asks for school leaving certificates (X and X+II) with subjects and grades, and degree certificates “with the Higher Education Commission (HEC) attestation stamps”. It does not say how the May 2026 HEC e-Attestation Certificate is treated. Last checked 29 October 2026.

Verified: we read the official page on the date shown. Needs review: the page could not be read automatically, was unclear or conflicted with another official page, or the information comes from press reports. Outdated: replaced by a newer official instruction.
