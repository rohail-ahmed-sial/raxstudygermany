German flashcards with smart revision

Spaced repetition for 1,506 words, writing phrases and 125 verbs.

Smart revision (spaced repetition): after each card, say how well you knew it. Cards you find easy come back after days or weeks; cards you miss come back in a few minutes. Ten to fifteen minutes a day is enough. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Choose a deck to start

Keyboard: Space turn over, 1–4 rate, L listen.

How smart revision works

  1. Look and recallRead the front and say the answer aloud before you turn the card.
  2. Rate honestlyAgain = you did not know it. Hard = you needed time. Good = you knew it. Easy = instant.
  3. Come back tomorrowThe deck shows the cards that are due today first, then new cards up to your daily limit.
