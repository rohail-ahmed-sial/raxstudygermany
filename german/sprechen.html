Goethe A1 and A2 Sprechen practice

Random exam cards, model answers with audio, a timer and a recorder.

Practise with random exam cards, just like in the test. Speak your answer aloud first, then open the model answer and listen. Record yourself to hear your progress; recordings never leave your device.

A1 Sprechen (Start Deutsch 1)

About 15 minutes in a group of up to four, no preparation time. Three parts worth 3, 6 and 6 points.

Teil 1: introduce yourself

Say something about each word: Name?, Alter?, Land?, Wohnort?, Sprachen?, Beruf?, Hobby? Then the examiner asks you to spell a word (for example your name) and say a number (for example your phone number).

Teil 2: ask and answer on a topic

You draw a card with a topic and a word. Ask your partner a question with the word, then answer their question.

Teil 3: requests

You draw a picture card. Make a polite request (Können Sie bitte …? / Gib mir bitte …) and react to your partner.

A2 Sprechen

About 15 minutes in pairs, no preparation time. A short warm-up is not marked.

Teil 1: questions about the person

Four word cards. Ask your partner a question with each card and answer their questions in full sentences.

Teil 2: talk about yourself

One topic card with four prompts. Speak for about a minute, then answer one or two questions from the examiner.

Teil 3: plan something together

You and your partner have different diaries. Make suggestions, say no politely, and agree on a time.
