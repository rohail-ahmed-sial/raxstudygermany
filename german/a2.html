Goethe-Zertifikat A2: exam pattern, marks and practice

Four modules, about 105 minutes, three pass rules.

Checked 29 October 2026

The Goethe-Zertifikat A2 shows that you can handle familiar everyday situations: short messages, simple conversations about yourself, shopping, work and travel. It is for adults; the teen version is called Goethe-Zertifikat A2: Fit in Deutsch. At A2 the exam is not modular: you must take all four parts, and only the overall result counts.

Lesen, Hören and Schreiben form the written exam (about 90 minutes in one block, 75 points). Sprechen is the oral exam (25 points); it is usually held in pairs, and lasts about 10 minutes if you are examined alone.

Pass mark and grades

You need all three: at least 60 of 100 points in total, at least 45 of 75 in the written exam and at least 15 of 25 in Sprechen. Miss one and the whole exam is failed.

What happens in each module

Lesen: 30 minutes, 20 tasks

  • Teil 1: a short newspaper text; choose a, b or c.
  • Teil 2: an information board (department store, town hall); where do you go? a, b or c.
  • Teil 3: an e-mail or letter; choose a, b or c.
  • Teil 4: match situations to short ads; for one situation there is no matching ad.

Practise A2 Lesen.

Hören: about 30 minutes, 20 tasks

  • Teil 1: short radio texts, announcements and phone messages; a, b or c. Played twice.
  • Teil 2: one conversation; match people or days to information. Played once.
  • Teil 3: five short everyday conversations; a, b or c. Played twice.
  • Teil 4: a radio interview; ja or nein. Played twice.

Practise A2 Hören.

Schreiben: 30 minutes

  • Teil 1: an SMS or short message to a friend (du), about 20–30 words, three points.
  • Teil 2: an e-mail to a teacher, boss, neighbour or company (Sie), about 30–40 words, three points.

Two examiners mark each text for task completion and language. Go to the Schreiben guide with 67 tasks.

Sprechen: about 15 minutes, in pairs

  • Teil 1 (about 3 min): with four word cards, ask your partner questions about their life and answer theirs.
  • Teil 2 (about 3 min each): talk about yourself on a topic card with four prompts, then answer one or two questions from the examiner.
  • Teil 3 (about 5 min): plan something together with your partner using two different diaries: make suggestions, agree and find a time.

There is no preparation time. A short warm-up conversation is not marked. Practise A2 Sprechen.

How to prepare

  1. Finish A1 firstA2 builds on every A1 word and structure.
  2. Learn the 691 new A2 wordsIn the vocabulary list and flashcards.
  3. Master the writing templatesSchreiben is the easiest module to improve fast: two templates and 40 phrases.
  4. Train listening dailyTen minutes of Hören every day beats one long session a week.
  5. Mock testsAim for 70+ with at least 50 in the written part before you book.
