Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (Start Deutsch 1): exam pattern, marks and practice

Four modules, about 80 minutes, 60 of 100 points to pass.

Checked 29 October 2026

The Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 shows that you can understand and use very simple everyday German: introduce yourself, ask and answer simple questions, read short notices and fill in a form. It is for adults; there is a separate version for teenagers (Fit in Deutsch 1).

Each module has 15 raw points, shown as up to 25 points on the result. The written exam (Hören, Lesen, Schreiben) is worth 75 points and the oral exam 25.

Pass mark and grades

You pass with at least 60 of 100 points and you must take every part. If you get fewer than 35 points in the written exam, you cannot reach 60 even with full marks in speaking. If you fail, you repeat the whole exam.

What happens in each module

Hören: about 20 minutes, 15 tasks

  • Teil 1: short everyday conversations; choose a, b or c (often with pictures). Played twice.
  • Teil 2: announcements at a station, airport or shop; richtig or falsch. Played once.
  • Teil 3: phone messages; choose a, b or c. Played twice.

You get about 3 minutes at the end to copy answers to the answer sheet. Practise A1 Hören.

Lesen: 25 minutes, 15 tasks

  • Teil 1: two short notes or e-mails; richtig or falsch.
  • Teil 2: for each situation, choose the right ad (a or b).
  • Teil 3: signs and notices; richtig or falsch.

Practise A1 Lesen.

Schreiben: 20 minutes

  • Teil 1: read a short text and fill in five gaps in a form (1 point each).
  • Teil 2: write a short message of about 30 words that answers three points (up to 3 points each, plus 1 point for the right form). Spelling mistakes only cost points if they make your text hard to understand.

Practise A1 Schreiben.

Sprechen: about 15 minutes, in a group of up to four

  • Teil 1: introduce yourself (name, age, country, home, languages, job, hobby), then spell a word and say a number. 3 points.
  • Teil 2: with word cards on a topic, ask a question and answer your partner. 6 points.
  • Teil 3: with picture cards, make a request and react to your partner’s request. 6 points.

There is no preparation time. Examiners judge whether you can be understood, not how many small mistakes you make. Practise A1 Sprechen.

How to prepare

  1. Learn the A1 wordsThe 815 A1 words in the vocabulary list, starting with the core words in the flashcards.
  2. Learn the basic grammarsein and haben, present tense, articles, accusative, negation, modal verbs.
  3. Practise each moduleOne Lesen or Hören set a day, plus your self-introduction out loud.
  4. Take mock testsUse the mock-test generator until you score 70+ twice in a row.
