125 German verbs for A1 and A2

Present tense, Perfekt and the English meaning for every verb.

The verbs you need to write and speak at A2. split marks separable verbs: the small part goes to the end (Ich bringe einen Kuchen mit). In the past column, ist means the verb uses sein (movement or change). The first 24 verbs are the ones that appear in exam instructions.
