German A1 and A2

Everything you need to pass the Goethe-Zertifikat A1 and A2, explained in plain English.

Start at A1 if you are a beginner, even if your goal is A2: the A2 exam assumes everything from A1. A realistic pace for a full-time learner is about two to three months per level with daily practice. Every page works on a phone, and your progress is saved in this browser.

Where A1 or A2 matters for a visa

German certificates are often requested in visa procedures, but the level depends on the route, and sometimes on the institution or employer. A certificate is never a guarantee of a visa. These are the levels named in the official guidance we checked:

RouteGerman level named in official guidanceDetails
Joining a spouse in GermanyA1 (certificate not older than one year when applying in Pakistan), with exceptionsFamily reunification
Opportunity Card (points route)A1 German or B2 English; more German gives more pointsOpportunity Card
Recognition partnershipA2Recognition routes
Vocational training (Ausbildung)Normally B1Ausbildung visa
Au pairA1Au pair
Studies taught in GermanDegree programmes usually about C1 (for example TestDaF or DSH); a Studienkolleg usually B1 or B2Student visa

Checked against official sources on 29 October 2026. Always confirm the current requirement on the official page for your route.

A 12-week plan from zero to A2

WeeksFocusEvery day (about 60 minutes)
1–2Sounds, numbers, greetings, sein and haben, present tense15 min flashcards (A1 core), 15 min pronunciation, 20 min grammar, 10 min Hören
3–4Articles, accusative, negation, modal verbs; A1 Lesen and Hören15 min flashcards, 20 min Lesen or Hören practice, 15 min grammar, 10 min speaking aloud
5–6A1 Schreiben (form + message), A1 Sprechen; first A1 mock test15 min flashcards, 25 min writing or speaking, 20 min mock-test parts
7–8Dative, separable verbs, Perfekt; A2 Lesen and Hören15 min flashcards (A2), 20 min practice, 15 min grammar, 10 min Sprechen cards
9–10A2 Schreiben: masters, phrase bank, 30 tasks15 min flashcards, 30 min Schreiben tasks with cover-and-write, 15 min Hören
11–12weil/dass, adjective endings, comparison; A2 mock tests15 min flashcards, one full mock test every 2–3 days, review mistakes
