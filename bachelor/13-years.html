Bachelor’s in Germany with 13 years of education

HSSC plus one completed year at a recognised university in Pakistan: what counts, which subjects open, and how to apply.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

Short answer. With your HSSC and one full, successfully completed year of undergraduate study at a recognised university in Pakistan, you can apply directly for a bachelor’s in Germany in the same or a related field, without a Studienkolleg. This is how DAAD Pakistan describes the route. The university, or uni-assist for it, checks your documents and decides. Verified

The university decides. This guide explains the general rules published by DAAD and uni-assist. Each university, or uni-assist on its behalf, checks your documents and makes the final decision. RAX Study Germany is not an admission service and does not rank or compare universities.

What counts as 13 years

In Pakistan, Matric takes 10 years and the HSSC (Intermediate) another two, so the HSSC means 12 years of education. The “13th year” is the first year of a bachelor’s programme at a university, usually two semesters with every course and exam passed. A year spent somewhere else does not automatically count.

Same or related field

Admission on this route is subject-restricted (in German, fachgebunden): it opens bachelor’s programmes that match what you studied in Pakistan, not every subject. Whether two subjects are “related” is the university’s decision. These examples show the idea; they are not guarantees:

First year in PakistanLikely related bachelor’s in GermanyUsually not covered
BS Computer Science or Software EngineeringComputer Science (Informatik), Business Informatics, Data ScienceMedicine, Law, Architecture
BS Electrical, Mechanical or Civil EngineeringEngineering programmes in the same branch, often also related engineering subjectsBusiness, humanities
BBA, B.Com or BS EconomicsBusiness Administration, Economics, International BusinessEngineering, natural sciences
BS Physics, Mathematics or ChemistryThe same science, often related sciences or engineeringMedicine, humanities

If you want a different subject in Germany, you have three options: apply in a field related to your year in Pakistan, study one year in the new field in Pakistan first, or take the Studienkolleg course that matches the new subject.

Do you still need German?

  • German-taught bachelor’s: yes. Most bachelor’s programmes in Germany are taught in German, and universities usually ask for German at about C1 before you enrol, for example TestDaF or DSH. Each programme states which certificates it accepts.
  • English-taught bachelor’s: you need the English proof the programme asks for, often IELTS or TOEFL. German is not required for admission, but it helps with everyday life and part-time work. IELTS practice.
  • Compared with the Studienkolleg: on the 13-year route you skip the Studienkolleg’s German entrance test, but the language requirement of your programme still applies.

Documents for the 13-year route

  • Matric (X) and HSSC (X+II) certificates with the overview of subjects and grades (uni-assist asks for both).
  • Transcript of your completed university year, showing every course and grade. HEC can attest an incomplete transcript if the university states your academic status.
  • A letter or certificate from the university confirming the year you completed, if the transcript does not show it clearly.
  • HEC attestation of your university documents. TU Dortmund, for example, asks applicants from Pakistan for HEC attestation on transcripts and degree certificates. Since May 2026 HEC issues an e-Attestation Certificate; ask uni-assist or the university whether they accept it. Needs review
  • IBCC attestation of Matric and HSSC documents if the university asks for it. IBCC guide.
  • Language certificate (German or English, as the programme requires), passport and any programme-specific documents such as a CV or motivation letter.

Never order an apostille for Germany: Germany objected to Pakistan’s accession, so a Pakistani apostille has no effect there. What Germany actually asks for.

Step-by-step plan

  1. Choose a related subjectPick bachelor’s programmes that match your first year in Pakistan. Use the DAAD admission database to check your qualification.
  2. Finish the year properlyPass every exam of both semesters. Keep your results cards and request the official transcript as soon as results are final.
  3. Prove your languageBook TestDaF or DSH (German-taught) or IELTS or TOEFL (English-taught) early: test dates fill up.
  4. Attest your documentsIBCC for school documents if required, HEC for your university transcript. Then scan everything clearly.
  5. Apply by the deadlineThrough uni-assist or directly, as each university says. Typical deadlines are 15 July (winter) and 15 January (summer), but check every programme.
  6. Admission, money, visaWith the admission letter, open a blocked account and apply for the student visa on the Consular Services Portal.

Common questions

Is 13 years enough for any subject?

No. It opens subjects that are the same as or related to your first year in Pakistan.

Do I start in the second year in Germany?

Usually you start in the first semester. Universities can recognise individual courses you already passed; ask after admission.

Do I need an APS certificate?

No. The APS checks applicants with qualifications from China, Vietnam and India; Pakistan is not included.

Does a year at a private university count?

It can, if the university is recognised by HEC and listed as recognised in anabin. Check before you rely on it.

Can I apply while my second-semester results are pending?

Some universities allow you to submit missing results later; many do not. Read the programme’s rules, and expect to need the final transcript before enrolment.

Official sources for this page

  • DAAD Pakistan: Getting started Verified
    The Pakistani HSSC “is not considered as equivalent to a German Secondary School Certificate (Abitur)”. Applicants “have to complete one year of undergraduate studies at a university in Pakistan” before applying for a bachelor’s in the same field, or attend a Studienkolleg and pass the Feststellungsprüfung. English-taught programmes usually ask for IELTS or TOEFL; German-taught programmes for a German language exam. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • DAAD Pakistan: Getting admitted to an undergraduate course at a German university (PDF, 2019 update) Verified
    An HSSC (FSc) certificate is not accepted as an entrance qualification. Without A-levels or at least one completed year of undergraduate study at a Pakistani university, you attend a Studienkolleg first. Courses M, T, W, G and S at universities and TI, WW, GD and SW at universities of applied sciences. German at about B1 and an admission test (mostly German and maths). Older document, consistent with DAAD’s current page. Last checked 29 October 2026.

    Interactive check of whether a school-leaving certificate (plus any university study) gives access to bachelor’s programmes. Results are guidance only; the university decides. We could not read results automatically, so run it yourself for your exact qualification. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • anabin: school-leaving qualifications with university access (KMK) Needs review
    The database of the Standing Conference of education ministers that universities use to classify foreign certificates and institutions. Interactive; not readable automatically. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • uni-assist: Pakistan country information Verified
    Asks for your school leaving certificate including an overview of subjects and grades (X and X+II), and degree certificates with HEC attestation. Does not say how the HEC e-Attestation Certificate (May 2026) is treated. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • TU Dortmund: Application information for non-EU/EEA bachelor’s applicants Verified
    Example deadlines: winter semester 15 July (German-taught) and 15 May (English-taught), summer semester 15 January, all through uni-assist. Applicants from Pakistan: HEC attestation on transcripts and degree certificate, uploaded as PDF. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • HEC: Degree attestation Verified
    Fully online e-Attestation, PKR 3,000 per document. Incomplete transcripts can be attested if the university states the academic status. Last checked 29 October 2026.

    The APS checks documents of applicants with Chinese, Vietnamese or Indian qualifications. Pakistan is not included. Last checked 29 October 2026.
