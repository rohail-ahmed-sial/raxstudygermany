Studienkolleg for Pakistani students (12 years)

The one-year foundation course in Germany for HSSC holders: course types, German level, entrance test and applying.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

A Studienkolleg is a preparatory course in Germany for international applicants whose school certificate does not give direct access to a German university, as is the case with the Pakistani HSSC. It usually lasts two semesters and ends with the assessment examination (Feststellungsprüfung). After passing it, you can apply for bachelor’s programmes in the subject area of your course.

The university decides. This guide explains the general rules published by DAAD and uni-assist. Each university, or uni-assist on its behalf, checks your documents and makes the final decision. RAX Study Germany is not an admission service and does not rank or compare universities.

Your future degree subject decides your course. Most applicants from Pakistan choose the T course.

Requirements

  • Your HSSC with the overview of subjects and grades, and your Matric certificate.
  • German: most Studienkollegs ask for about B1 (Kassel: a B1 certificate or a completed B1 course with at least 400 lessons). Some ask for more: FU Berlin requires at least B2 and recommends C1.
  • An entrance test: usually German and, for the T and W courses, maths. Kassel uses a German C-test and middle-school maths; FU Berlin ranks applicants by score and holds the test in Berlin, not online.
  • Teaching is entirely in German, even if you later plan an English-taught degree.

How to apply

  1. Choose a university with a StudienkollegAt many universities you apply for a degree programme (often through uni-assist) and the university assigns you to its Studienkolleg.
  2. Reach the German levelTake a recognised exam such as Goethe, telc or ÖSD at B1 or B2.
  3. Apply by the deadlineDeadlines differ: for the 2026/27 winter intake Kassel’s deadline was 1 June 2026, FU Berlin’s period ran from 1 June to 15 July, with no summer intake.
  4. Take the entrance testIf invited. Many tests are held at the Studienkolleg in Germany; check whether you can take it remotely.
  5. VisaAdmission to a Studienkolleg is a basis for the student visa. You need the blocked account like any student. Student visa guide.

Things to know

  • Places are limited and competitive. Apply to more than one Studienkolleg if the rules allow it (FU Berlin asks you to apply through one university only).
  • Studienkollegs change: Kassel takes its last intake in 2026/27. Always check the Studienkolleg’s own page.
  • Public Studienkollegs are part of a university; private ones charge tuition. Check fees on the Studienkolleg’s page.
  • Some places, such as FU Berlin, also offer the Feststellungsprüfung as an external exam without attending the course.
  • If you have, or can get, one completed year at a recognised university in Pakistan, the 13-year route avoids the Studienkolleg for related subjects.

Sources

Official sources for this page

  • DAAD Pakistan: Getting started Verified
    The Pakistani HSSC “is not considered as equivalent to a German Secondary School Certificate (Abitur)”. Applicants “have to complete one year of undergraduate studies at a university in Pakistan” before applying for a bachelor’s in the same field, or attend a Studienkolleg and pass the Feststellungsprüfung. English-taught programmes usually ask for IELTS or TOEFL; German-taught programmes for a German language exam. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • DAAD Pakistan: Getting admitted to an undergraduate course at a German university (PDF, 2019 update) Verified
    An HSSC (FSc) certificate is not accepted as an entrance qualification. Without A-levels or at least one completed year of undergraduate study at a Pakistani university, you attend a Studienkolleg first. Courses M, T, W, G and S at universities and TI, WW, GD and SW at universities of applied sciences. German at about B1 and an admission test (mostly German and maths). Older document, consistent with DAAD’s current page. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • University of Kassel: Studienkolleg application and admittance Verified
    You apply for a degree programme through uni-assist and are assigned to the Studienkolleg. German B1 certificate (or a completed B1 course with at least 400 lessons). Entrance test: German C-test and maths. Deadline for winter 2026/27: 1 June 2026. The Studienkolleg Kassel takes its last intake in 2026/27. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Freie Universität Berlin: Studienkolleg application Verified
    Apply through one of FU Berlin, Humboldt University or Charité, 1 June to 15 July, winter intake only. B2 minimum, C1 recommended. Entry test on site in Berlin; ranking by score. An external Feststellungsprüfung without the course is also possible. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Make it in Germany: Visa for studying Verified
    Student visa for admission to a degree programme, Studienkolleg or preparatory course; proof of financing; work rules. Last checked 29 October 2026.
