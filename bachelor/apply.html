How to apply for a bachelor’s in Germany

uni-assist, deadlines, documents, costs and the student visa.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

The steps are the same for both routes: check that your education gives access, prove the language, attest your documents, apply by the deadline, then arrange money and the visa.

The university decides. This guide explains the general rules published by DAAD and uni-assist. Each university, or uni-assist on its behalf, checks your documents and makes the final decision. RAX Study Germany is not an admission service and does not rank or compare universities.

Step by step

  1. Check your accessUse the path checker, then the DAAD admission database for your exact qualification.
  2. Find programmesUse DAAD’s Study in Germany programme search or the Hochschulkompass. Read each programme’s admission rules, language requirement and deadline.
  3. Prove the languageGerman-taught: usually C1 level, for example TestDaF or DSH. English-taught: IELTS or TOEFL as the programme asks. Studienkolleg: about B1 or B2 German.
  4. Attest and scan your documentsIBCC for Matric and HSSC if required, HEC for university transcripts and degrees. No apostille for Germany.
  5. ApplyMany universities use uni-assist: register, select programmes, upload documents and pay. Some universities take applications directly. For admission-restricted bachelor’s programmes, some also require registration at hochschulstart.de.
  6. Admission and visaWith your admission or Studienkolleg admission, open a blocked account and apply for the student visa on the Consular Services Portal. Student visa guide.

uni-assist

  • uni-assist pre-checks international applications for many German universities. You upload your documents once and select programmes.
  • Fees: EUR 75 for the first application and EUR 30 for each further application (as listed by Hochschule Düsseldorf). Your bank may add transfer charges.
  • uni-assist needs several weeks to check an application, so apply early, not on the deadline day.
  • For Pakistan, uni-assist asks for the subject and grade overview for X (Matric) and X+II (HSSC), and HEC attestation on degree certificates.

Checklist

  • Matric and HSSC certificates and marks sheets, scanned clearly
  • University transcript for your completed year (13-year route)
  • HEC and, if required, IBCC attestation
  • Language certificate
  • Passport
  • CV and motivation letter if a programme asks
  • Application through uni-assist or the university, with fee paid
  • Admission letter, then blocked account and visa

For the visa documents, use the student visa checklist.

Official sources for this page

  • Hochschule Düsseldorf: Application via uni-assist Verified
    uni-assist fees: EUR 75 for the first application and EUR 30 for each further application. For admission-restricted bachelor’s programmes, also register at hochschulstart.de. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • TU Dortmund: Application information for non-EU/EEA bachelor’s applicants Verified
    Example deadlines: winter semester 15 July (German-taught) and 15 May (English-taught), summer semester 15 January, all through uni-assist. Applicants from Pakistan: HEC attestation on transcripts and degree certificate, uploaded as PDF. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • uni-assist: Pakistan country information Verified
    Asks for your school leaving certificate including an overview of subjects and grades (X and X+II), and degree certificates with HEC attestation. Does not say how the HEC e-Attestation Certificate (May 2026) is treated. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Heidelberg University: Financing your studies (Baden-Württemberg tuition fees) Verified
    Non-EU students in Baden-Württemberg pay EUR 1,500 per semester for bachelor’s and consecutive master’s programmes (page updated September 2025). Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • University of Kassel: Studienkolleg application and admittance Verified
    You apply for a degree programme through uni-assist and are assigned to the Studienkolleg. German B1 certificate (or a completed B1 course with at least 400 lessons). Entrance test: German C-test and maths. Deadline for winter 2026/27: 1 June 2026. The Studienkolleg Kassel takes its last intake in 2026/27. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Freie Universität Berlin: Studienkolleg application Verified
    Apply through one of FU Berlin, Humboldt University or Charité, 1 June to 15 July, winter intake only. B2 minimum, C1 recommended. Entry test on site in Berlin; ranking by score. An external Feststellungsprüfung without the course is also possible. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • DAAD: Database on admission requirements Needs review
    Interactive check of whether a school-leaving certificate (plus any university study) gives access to bachelor’s programmes. Results are guidance only; the university decides. We could not read results automatically, so run it yourself for your exact qualification. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Make it in Germany: Visa for studying Verified
    Student visa for admission to a degree programme, Studienkolleg or preparatory course; proof of financing; work rules. Last checked 29 October 2026.
