Bachelor’s in Germany from Pakistan

12 or 13 years of education: which route applies to you, what it costs and how long it takes.

Last checked: 29 October 2026 Verified

To start a bachelor’s degree in Germany you need a school-leaving qualification that German universities accept as a university entrance qualification (Hochschulzugangsberechtigung). DAAD Pakistan states that the Pakistani Higher Secondary School Certificate is not equivalent to the German Abitur. That leaves two common routes for applicants from Pakistan, and which one you take depends mainly on how many years of education you have completed.

12years

HSSC (Intermediate)

FSc, ICS, FA, I.Com or other HSSC. Not equal to the German Abitur: you go through a Studienkolleg first.

13years

HSSC + one year at university

One full, passed year of a bachelor’s at a recognised university in Pakistan: apply directly for a bachelor’s in the same or a related field. Read the guide.

16years

Completed bachelor’s

You can usually apply for a master’s. Applying for a second bachelor’s is also possible; check the programme.

The university decides. This guide explains the general rules published by DAAD and uni-assist. Each university, or uni-assist on its behalf, checks your documents and makes the final decision. RAX Study Germany is not an admission service and does not rank or compare universities.

From decision to first semester

  1. 12–18 months beforeChoose your route (Studienkolleg or the 13-year route) and subject area. Start German if you will study in German or need a Studienkolleg.
  2. 9–12 months beforeShortlist programmes with the official search tools, check each programme’s language and document rules, and book language tests.
  3. 6–9 months beforeGet your documents attested (IBCC for Matric and HSSC, HEC for university transcripts) and prepare scans.
  4. By the deadlineApply through uni-assist or directly. Many universities use 15 July for the winter semester and 15 January for the summer semester, but deadlines vary. How to apply.
  5. After admissionOpen a blocked account, apply for the student visa, then travel, register your address and enrol. Student visa guide.

Sources

Official sources for this page

  • DAAD Pakistan: Getting started Verified
    The Pakistani HSSC “is not considered as equivalent to a German Secondary School Certificate (Abitur)”. Applicants “have to complete one year of undergraduate studies at a university in Pakistan” before applying for a bachelor’s in the same field, or attend a Studienkolleg and pass the Feststellungsprüfung. English-taught programmes usually ask for IELTS or TOEFL; German-taught programmes for a German language exam. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • DAAD Pakistan: Getting admitted to an undergraduate course at a German university (PDF, 2019 update) Verified
    An HSSC (FSc) certificate is not accepted as an entrance qualification. Without A-levels or at least one completed year of undergraduate study at a Pakistani university, you attend a Studienkolleg first. Courses M, T, W, G and S at universities and TI, WW, GD and SW at universities of applied sciences. German at about B1 and an admission test (mostly German and maths). Older document, consistent with DAAD’s current page. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • DAAD: Database on admission requirements Needs review
    Interactive check of whether a school-leaving certificate (plus any university study) gives access to bachelor’s programmes. Results are guidance only; the university decides. We could not read results automatically, so run it yourself for your exact qualification. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • uni-assist: Pakistan country information Verified
    Asks for your school leaving certificate including an overview of subjects and grades (X and X+II), and degree certificates with HEC attestation. Does not say how the HEC e-Attestation Certificate (May 2026) is treated. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Hochschule Düsseldorf: Application via uni-assist Verified
    uni-assist fees: EUR 75 for the first application and EUR 30 for each further application. For admission-restricted bachelor’s programmes, also register at hochschulstart.de. Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • Heidelberg University: Financing your studies (Baden-Württemberg tuition fees) Verified
    Non-EU students in Baden-Württemberg pay EUR 1,500 per semester for bachelor’s and consecutive master’s programmes (page updated September 2025). Last checked 29 October 2026.
  • TU Dortmund: Application information for non-EU/EEA bachelor’s applicants Verified
    Example deadlines: winter semester 15 July (German-taught) and 15 May (English-taught), summer semester 15 January, all through uni-assist. Applicants from Pakistan: HEC attestation on transcripts and degree certificate, uploaded as PDF. Last checked 29 October 2026.
