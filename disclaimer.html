Disclaimer and privacy

What this site is, what it is not, and what it stores.

RAX Study Germany is an independent educational and visa-information resource. It is not operated by the German government, German Missions, Goethe-Institut, IELTS, or any immigration authority. Information is provided for general preparation and must be verified against the official requirements applicable to your circumstances. RAX Study Germany does not guarantee visa eligibility, appointment availability, processing time, or approval.

RAX Study Germany is an independent educational and information platform. It is not affiliated with IBCC, HEC, Pakistan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the German government, Goethe-Institut, or IELTS. Document-attestation requirements depend on document type, destination, and the receiving authority. Always confirm current requirements through the official authority before submitting or paying for an application.

Not legal advice

Nothing on this site is legal advice. Visa rules depend on your nationality, where you live, your documents and the decision of the authorities. Requirements, fees and procedures change, often without notice. For a binding answer, ask the German mission responsible for you, the foreigners’ authority in Germany, or a qualified lawyer.

Exams and trademarks

Goethe-Zertifikat is a product of the Goethe-Institut. IELTS is jointly owned by the British Council, IDP IELTS and Cambridge University Press & Assessment. All practice tasks on this site are original and are not official exam material. Scores from the mock tests are estimates.

Privacy

RAX Study Germany has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. It does not collect or send your answers anywhere.

  • Saved in your browser. Your practice results, flashcard schedule, writing drafts, checklists, visa plans and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device. You can export, import or delete them on the My progress page. Clearing your browser data also deletes them.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Barlow typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers. Whoever hosts a copy of this site can self-host the fonts instead (see the README).
  • Speech. Read-aloud uses your browser’s built-in text-to-speech voices. Depending on your browser and operating system, some voices may be provided online by that vendor.
  • Microphone. The speaking recorder asks for permission and keeps the recording in your browser only. It is never uploaded.
  • Hosting. The host of the site (for example Netlify) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

External links

We link to official websites so that you can check information at its source. We are not responsible for their content.
